# **The Universal Mirror: An Exhaustive Cartography of Natural Correspondences in the Theology of Emanuel Swedenborg**

## **1\. Introduction: The Physics of Spiritual Causality**

The natural world, in its vast and intricate complexity, is not merely a stage upon which human history unfolds, nor is it a random aggregation of matter governed by blind mechanical forces. In the theological physics of Emanuel Swedenborg (1688–1772), the physical universe is "a theater representative of the Lord’s kingdom" (*AC* §3483). It is a mirror—"from the things that exist in the natural world the things of the spiritual world can be seen as in a mirror" (*HH* §56)—reflecting the infinite nuances of Divine Love and Wisdom through the finite forms of matter. This worldview is predicated on the "Doctrine of Correspondences," a hermeneutic and metaphysical framework asserting that every physical object, elemental force, and biological organism is an "ultimate" effect caused and sustained by a specific spiritual reality.  
To understand the Swedenborgian map of the cosmos is to understand a universe of "influx." The spiritual world, which is the realm of causes, flows continuously into the natural world, which is the realm of effects. Without this sustaining influx the natural world could not continue, for "nothing can subsist from itself, but only from that which is prior to itself, thus from a First, and if separated from that it would utterly perish and vanish" (*HH* §106); "preservation is perpetual creation, as subsistence is perpetual existence" (*TCR* §46). Consequently, the study of nature is, in its essence, the study of the spiritual topography of the human mind and the Divine nature. Every ecosystem, from the arid silence of the desert to the teeming biodiversity of the ocean, corresponds to a specific state of affection (love) and understanding (truth).  
This report presents a comprehensive cartography of these correspondences. Relying exclusively on Swedenborg’s theological writings—principally *Arcana Coelestia*, *Apocalypse Revealed*, *Apocalypse Explained*, *Heaven and Hell*, and *Divine Love and Wisdom*—we map the external physical world. We exclude the correspondence of the human anatomy to focus entirely on the macrocosm: the elemental substrate, the mineral foundations, the hydrological cycles, the botanical landscape, and the zoological kingdom. This analysis reveals a dynamic, living symbolism where "Gold" is not merely a metal but the heavy, malleable substance of Celestial Love; where a "Lion" is not merely a predator but the roaring power of Divine Truth; and where the "Sun" is the primary icon of the Lord Himself.

### **1.1 The Principle of Discrete Degrees and Influx**

The architecture of this correspondence is hierarchical. Swedenborg distinguishes between the "Natural," "Spiritual," and "Celestial" degrees of reality. These are not continuous degrees—like the shading of light into dark—but "discrete" degrees, "like things prior, subsequent and final; or like end, cause, and effect" (*DLW* §184).

* **The Celestial Degree:** Corresponds to the highest heaven, the "Will" faculty, and the motive of Love.  
* **The Spiritual Degree:** Corresponds to the middle heaven, the "Understanding" faculty, and the motive of Wisdom or Truth.  
* **The Natural Degree:** Corresponds to the physical world (and the lowest heaven), the realm of Action and Effect.

Everything in nature exists as a vessel for these higher degrees. A tree, for instance, has a natural form, but trees signify perceptions and knowledges (*AC* §103), and all "the fruit of faith" is "the fruit of the good of love and charity" (*AC* §3146).

### **1.2 The Duality of Meaning: Genuine and Opposite**

A critical feature of Swedenborgian cartography is the "Principle of Opposites." Because humanity possesses free will and has, historically, turned away from the Divine flow (the "Fall"), the natural world acts as a vocabulary that can describe both heaven and hell. Every correspondence has a **Genuine Sense** (relating to the Lord, heaven, and regeneration) and an **Opposite Sense** (relating to the selfhood or "the self," hell, and degeneration).

* **The Sun:** In the genuine sense, it is Divine Love. In the opposite sense, it is the burning fire of self-love (*AC* §2441).  
* **The Lion:** In the genuine sense, it is "truth in power from good" (The Lion of Judah). In the opposite sense, it is "the evil of the love of self in its power" (*AC* §§6367, 6441).

This report will systematically explore both poles of these correspondences, providing a complete map of the spiritual moral landscape overlaid upon the physical world.

## **2\. The Primal Forces: Heat, Light, and Atmospheres**

The genesis of all correspondence lies in the physics of light and heat. In Swedenborg’s system, these are not secondary byproducts of chemical combustion but the primary "substances" of the universe, emanating directly from the interaction between the Spiritual Sun and the Natural Sun.

### **2.1 The Two Suns and the Origin of Energy**

The cosmos is binary. There is a sun of the spiritual world and a sun of the natural world. This distinction is the pivot upon which all life turns.

* **The Spiritual Sun:** This is the first emanation of the Divine. It is not God Himself in His essence (which is infinite and unapproachable), but "the first proceeding" from Him (*DLW* §93). To the angels, the Lord appears as this Sun.  
  * **Correspondence:** The Lord.  
  * **Heat:** Corresponds to **Divine Love**. Just as physical heat warms, expands, and sustains biological life, Divine Love warms the human will, expands the affections, and constitutes the vital "heat" of life.  
  * **Light:** Corresponds to **Divine Wisdom**. Just as physical light illuminates the eye and enables navigation, Divine Wisdom illuminates the understanding and enables spiritual perception.  
* **The Natural Sun:** This is pure fire and is described by Swedenborg as "dead" (*DLW* §157). It was created "to render aid, as a kind of substitute" (*DLW* §153), "to this end, that in outmosts all things may be fixed, settled, and constant" (*DLW* §165).  
  * **Genuine Sense:** The service of the natural to the spiritual; the auxiliary forces of nature supporting the growth of the mind.  
  * **Opposite Sense:** **Self-Love.** When worship is directed to nature or the natural sun, it corresponds to the worship of self (*AC* §2441). When the angel who pours his vial upon the sun is given "to scorch men with fire" (Rev 16:8), it is the Lord's love tormenting those who "were in the lusts of evils" (*AR* §691).

### **2.2 The Atmospheres: The Medium of Transmission**

Energy requires a medium. Swedenborg details a physics of "atmospheres" that descend from the Spiritual Sun down to the earth (*DLW* §§173–176). These distinct layers of gaseous or ethereal substances correspond to the levels of the human mind and the heavens: "the angels of the highest heaven live as in a pure aura, the angels of the middle heaven as it were in an ether, and the angels of the lowest heaven as it were in air" (*Divine Wisdom* §166; *AE* §726).

| Atmosphere | Spiritual Correspondence | Associated Heaven | Faculty of Mind |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Aura** | Divine Love, Inmost Peace | Celestial (Third) | The Will (Inmost Affection) |
| **Ether** | Divine Wisdom, Spiritual Truth | Spiritual (Second) | The Understanding (Intellect) |
| **Air** | Thought, Perception, Action | Natural (First) | The Natural Mind/Sensory |

* **Aura:** The highest, most subtle atmosphere. It is the immediate vessel of Divine Love and corresponds to the celestial sphere of the mind—the unconscious or super-conscious level where love is felt as a state of peace and innocence.  
* **Ether:** The middle atmosphere, associated with the transmission of light (optics). It corresponds to the rational or spiritual mind, where truths are seen and understood. Sight requires "an atmosphere purer than air", and thought and affection "still purer atmospheres" (*DLW* §176).  
* **Air:** The lowest atmosphere, associated with sound (acoustics) and respiration.  
  * **Sound:** Corresponds to the affection of thought. Speech is thought taking form in air: "the tones of their speech corresponding to their affections, and the vocal articulations which are words corresponding to the ideas of thought" (*HH* §236).  
  * **Respiration (Breath):** Corresponds to the life of the **Understanding** (Truth/Faith). Just as the lungs purify the blood, the understanding purifies the will (heart) by teaching truth (*DLW* §§371, 420). Swedenborg asserts that in the "Most Ancient Church," humanity possessed "internal respiration," a "respiration concordant with and similar to that of angels" (*AC* §§97, 805). When it ceased, "external respiration gradually succeeded," and with it "a language of words" (*AC* §608); and because man would otherwise "perish eternally," the Lord "provided that the will should be separated from the understanding" (*AC* §640).

### **2.3 Atmospheric Phenomena and Weather**

The variation of the atmosphere corresponds to the fluctuating states of the human spirit. The "weather of the soul" is a literal correspondence to the weather of the earth.

* **Wind:** Corresponds to the **influx of spiritual influence**—from heaven, and in the opposite sense from hell (*AR* §343).  
  * *Mechanism:* Just as wind is air in motion, spiritual influx is thought in activity.  
  * *The Four Winds:* Signify the influx of the heavens from all quarters (Good and Truth).  
  * *Storms/Tempests:* Correspond to **Temptation**. A storm is a disturbance in the atmosphere; temptation is a disturbance in the mind caused by the influx of falsities attacking truths (*AC* §§739, 842). To "hold back the winds" (Rev 7:1) is "a withholding and restraining by the Lord of a nearer and thence more powerful influx into the lower parts, where the good were conjoined with the evil" (*AR* §343).  
* **Mist:** Corresponds to the **falsities of evil**—"a mist or thick cloud to falsities from that love," the love of self (*HH* §585)—or the obscurity of the natural mind. Mist rises from the ground (the sensory self) and obscures the sun (the Lord). It signifies the "fallacies of the senses"—beliefs based solely on physical appearance that cloud spiritual reality.  
* **Clouds:** Correspond to the **Literal Sense of the Word**.  
  * *Mechanism:* Clouds temper the heat and light of the sun, making it endurable. Similarly, the literal stories of the Bible (the cloud) veil the intense brilliance of the internal spiritual sense (the sun) so that man is not blinded or overwhelmed. The "Son of Man coming in the clouds" signifies "that the Lord is about to reveal himself in the Word through the internal sense" (*AE* §36).  
* **Rainbows:** The bow set in the cloud (Gen 9:13) signifies "the state of the regenerated spiritual man, which is like a rainbow" (*AC* §1042). The cloud is the obscure light of the spiritual man, the own of his understanding; "when innocence, charity, and mercy are insinuated into this own by the Lord," it appears "as an appearance of truth, together with truth from the Lord," and so as a coloured bow—the token of the covenant (*AC* §1043).

## **3\. The Lithosphere: The Mineral Foundations**

Descending from the active atmospheres, we reach the passive, fixed matter of the earth. The mineral kingdom corresponds to the "Ultimate" or "Natural" degree—the hard, enduring facts and loves that form the foundation of existence.

### **3.1 Geology and the Structure of Truth**

The earth itself corresponds to the **Church** or the **External Man** (*AC* §§278, 566, 662). It is the container for the biological life (spiritual growth) that takes root within it.

* **Rock:** Corresponds to **The Lord as to Divine Truth** (specifically the truth of faith) (*AC* §8581).  
  * *The Rock of Ages:* Represents the unshakeable nature of Divine Truth.  
  * *Clefts in the Rock:* Correspond to "the obscurity and falsity of faith such as is with those who are in external things without what is internal" (*AC* §10582).
  * *Opposite Sense:* Falsity in general, and from its hardness the hardened heart ("They have made their faces harder than a rock," Jer 5:3). In the spiritual world, those who are in the doctrinals of their church with some charity dwell upon rocks with level places, while those who have been in "faith alone" "dwell within the rocks, in caverns and cells there" (*AE* §411).  
* **Stone:** Corresponds to **Natural Truth** or "Scientifics" (facts) (*AC* §§643, 1298).  
  * *Mechanism:* Stones are used to build. Facts are the stones with which the mind builds its system of doctrines.  
  * *Hewn Stone vs. Unhewn Stone:* Unhewn stones (altars) represent truths from the Divine that are not altered by self-intelligence. Hewn stones represent doctrines fashioned by human reasoning—"self-intelligence" (*AC* §8941).  
* **Dust:** Corresponds to the **lowest natural**, the corporeal, and the damned (*AC* §§249, 278).  
  * *Meaning:* Dust is earth that has no cohesion and no life. It corresponds to the state of the "damned" or the "self" (selfhood) which is dry and disconnected from heaven. "To shake the dust off one’s feet" signifies "that what is cursed therefrom might not cling to them" (*AE* §365).  
* **Sand:** Corresponds to **Scientifics (knowledge) without life** (*AC* §2850).  
  * *Meaning:* Sand is stone broken into tiny, incoherent particles. It corresponds to knowledge stored in the memory that is not organized into a life of use. The "sand of the sea" signifies the vast, innumerable quantity of natural facts that form the boundary of the intellect but are not spiritual in themselves. A house built on sand is a life built on "faith separate from charity" (*AE* §212).  
* **Salt:** Corresponds to the **desire for conjunction** between Truth and Good.  
  * *Mechanism:* Salt gives flavor and preserves. Spiritually, truth without love is insipid and decays. The desire to unite truth with love is the "salt" of the earth. "Salt losing its savor" is truth that has lost its affection for good (*AC* §§9207, 10300).

### **3.2 The Deep History of Metals: The Four Ages**

Swedenborg explicitly links the hierarchy of metals to the spiritual history of humanity, referenced as the Four Ages (Golden, Silver, Copper, Iron) and depicted in Nebuchadnezzar’s dream (Daniel 2) (*TCR* §762). This is not merely archaeological but ontological—it describes the descending quality of the human mind.

#### **3.2.1 Gold (The Celestial Degree)**

* **Core Correspondence:** **Celestial Good**; the Good of Love to the Lord (*AC* §§113, 425).  
* **Properties:** Gold is the most heavy, precious, and ductile of metals. Its weight corresponds to the "gravitas" or importance of love. Its color corresponds to fire (love).  
* **Context:** The "Golden Age" (Most Ancient Church) was a state of innocence where humans were led directly by love. The Ark of the Covenant was overlaid with gold, "to lay the foundation upon good" (*AC* §9490).
* **Opposite Sense:** **The Evil of Self-Love**; gold signifies "good, and in the opposite sense evil" (*AC* §9146). Babylon is described as having a "golden cup," symbolizing the profanation of the highest good for the lowest end (dominion) (*AR* §725).

#### **3.2.2 Silver (The Spiritual Degree)**

* **Core Correspondence:** **Spiritual Truth**; the Truth derived from Good (*AC* §§425, 1551).  
* **Properties:** Silver is white and reflective, corresponding to the nature of truth which reflects the light of Divine Wisdom.  
* **Context:** The "Silver Age" (Ancient Church) was a state where direct perception of love was lost, and humanity began to be led by conscience and the precepts of faith (Truth).  
* **Opposite Sense:** **Falsity** that appears like truth, "for they who are in falsity think that falsity is truth" (*AC* §1551). Spiritual counterfeiting.

#### **3.2.3 Copper/Brass (The Natural-Celestial Degree)**

* **Core Correspondence:** **Natural Good**; the Good of Charity and external kindness (*AC* §425).  
* **Properties:** A base metal, reddish in color (linking it to fire/love), used for tools and vessels. It corresponds to the "neighborly love" of the natural man—kindness, morality, and good works that are external but genuine.  
* **Context:** The "Copper Age" corresponds to a state of natural goodness and simple obedience.

#### **3.2.4 Iron (The Natural-Spiritual Degree)**

* **Core Correspondence:** **Natural Truth**; Truth in its ultimate form; Scientific or Literal Truth (*AC* §425).  
* **Properties:** Hard, rigid, brittle, used for weapons and strong structures. It corresponds to the strict adherence to rules, laws, and facts without the softening influence of high charity.  
* **Context:** The "Iron Age" is the lowest state of the church, where spiritual perception is lost, and only the hard, literal truth remains. The "legs of iron" in Daniel represent a church that stands on mere doctrine and authority.  
* **Opposite Sense:** **Hard Falsity**: iron signifies "truth in ultimates, and likewise falsity there, and at the same time what is hard" (*AE* §557). The "rod of iron" with which the nations are ruled signifies the power of literal truth, "truths from the literal sense of the Word, and at the same time … rational things from natural light" (*AR* §148).

### **3.3 Topography: The Moral Elevation**

In Swedenborg’s physics, "altitude" corresponds to "degree of state." High places correspond to internal/spiritual states; low places to external/natural states.

* **Mountains:** Correspond to **Celestial Love** (Love to the Lord) (*AC* §6435).  
  * *Mechanism:* Mountains are elevated above the earth and are the first to catch the light of the sun. They correspond to the "highest" state of the mind which is nearest to the Lord.  
  * *The Seven Mountains (Rev 17):* In the opposite sense, these signify the **Profanation of Love**: the Divine goods of the Word upon which Babylon's religious persuasion is founded, "destroyed in time, and at length profaned" (*AR* §737). When the love of self (Babylon) exalts itself to the height of heaven, it becomes a profane mountain.  
* **Hills:** Correspond to **Spiritual Love** (Charity toward the neighbor) (*AC* §6435). They are lower than mountains, corresponding to the "lesser" but still elevated good of charity.  
* **Valleys:** Correspond to the **Natural Mind** or the "External Man"; a valley signifies "lower, or what is the same, exterior things" (*AC* §3417).  
  * *Mechanism:* Valleys receive the runoff from the mountains. The natural mind is the vessel that receives the influx of good and truth from the internal spiritual mind. A "fruitful valley" is a natural mind rich in good works. A "dark valley" is a natural mind devoid of spiritual light.  
* **Caves/Caverns:** Correspond to **Obscurity** and the **Sensual Mind**.  
  * *Context:* In the spiritual world, the entrances to the hells often appear as dark caves or clefts in the rock (*HH* §§584–585). This corresponds to the desire of evil spirits (and the sensual mind) to hide from the Divine Light and dwell in the darkness of their own falsities. To "hide in the caves" (Rev 6\) corresponds to the terror of the evil when the light of truth reveals their internal state, for they "were now in evils and in the falsities of evil" (*AR* §338).

## **4\. The Hydrosphere: The Fluidity of Truth**

If the earth is the will/good, then water is the understanding/truth. Water cleanses, quenches thirst, and acts as a medium for transport—exact analogies for the functions of truth in the human spirit.

### **4.1 Bodies of Water and Intelligence**

* **The Ocean/Sea:** Corresponds to the **General Collection of Scientifics** (External Truths) and the **Literal Sense of the Word**.  
  * *Mechanism:* The sea is the gathering place of all waters. It corresponds to the memory, which is the gathering place of all knowledge (*AC* §28). It is the boundary of the land (church).  
  * *Opposite Sense:* **Hell.** The sea can represent a chaotic abyss of falsities and lusts. The "beast rising out of the sea" (Rev 13\) signifies "reasonings from the natural man, because the 'sea' out of which that beast came up signifies the natural of man" (*AE* §815).  
* **Rivers:** Correspond to **Intelligence** and the **Flow of Wisdom** (*AC* §108).  
  * *Mechanism:* A river flowing through a land corresponds to the influx of truth organizing and enlivening the mind.  
  * *River Euphrates:* Corresponds to **memory-knowledge**, "which is the ultimate or boundary" (*AC* §118); the reasoning mind is the Hiddekel. As the boundary of the land of Canaan (the spiritual church) it marks the extension of celestial things (*AC* §1866). Its waters, drunk "in the way of Assyria," denote **False Reasoning**—"falsities from reasonings" (*AC* §790).  
* **Fountains/Springs:** Correspond to the **Word** and the **Lord** as the Source of Truth (*AC* §2702).  
  * *Meaning:* A spring is the origin of the river. It represents the Lord as the source of all "Living Water" (Internal Truth).  
* **Wells:** Correspond to the **Literal Sense of the Word**.  
  * *Mechanism:* Water in a well is deep and must be drawn up with effort. This corresponds to the study required to extract doctrine from the literal text of the Bible: a well is "the Word as to the literal sense in which is the internal sense" (*AC* §3424).  
* **Pools:** Correspond to **Intelligence** derived from knowledges; a "pool of waters" signifies "the knowledges of truth" (*AE* §405).
  * *Context:* A "pool" is collected water (knowledge) available for use. The "Pool of Siloam" signifies "the Word in the letter," and to be washed there "is to be purified from falsities and evils" (*AE* §239).  
* **Marshes/Miry Places:** Correspond to **Profanation** and **Falsities**: the merely natural, which, "separate from spiritual life, is defiled by falsities and evils, which are 'miry places' and 'marshes'" (*AE* §342).  
  * *Mechanism:* Stagnant water breeds disease. Knowledge that is not used for life, or truth mixed with evil, becomes a "spiritual marsh"—a state of profanation where truth is suffocated by lust.

### **4.2 Hydrological Cycles and Instruction**

* **Rain:** Corresponds to **Divine Truth descending from Heaven**.  
  * *Meaning:* Just as rain waters the earth to produce crops, Divine Truth flows into the mind to produce the "fruit" of good works; in the genuine sense rain "signifies a blessing, and therefore also salvation," in the opposite sense damnation (*AC* §2445).
  * *Early and Latter Rain:* Correspond to "the influx and reception of Divine truth" when the man of the church is in his spiritual state and when he is in his natural state, for he "is by turns in a spiritual state and in a natural state" (*AE* §644).  
* **Flood/Inundation:** Corresponds to **Temptation** and **Suffocation**.  
  * *Mechanism:* A flood destroys boundaries and drowns life. Spiritually, a flood is an overwhelming influx of falsities and lusts that drown the perception of truth; floods signify both temptations and desolations (*AC* §705). Swedenborg interprets the Great Flood (Genesis) not as a physical deluge—the history there is "made-up history" (*AC* §1020)—but as the spiritual suffocation of the posterity of the "Most Ancient Church," whose "persuasions of falsity … extinguished all truth and good" (*AC* §660).  
* **Dew:** Corresponds to the **Truth of Peace** and **Innocence**: "the truth of good which is from a state of innocence and peace" (*AC* §3579).  
  * *Context:* Dew forms in the quiet of the morning. It represents the gentle, almost imperceptible influx of celestial truth that prepares the mind for the "heat" of the day (love/temptation). It is the instruction of the innocent.  
* **Snow:** Corresponds to **truth in ultimates**.
  * *Meaning:* "'Snow' means truth in ultimates by reason of the water of which it is composed, and its whiteness and brightness" (*AE* §67). Because it is white, it signifies the **purity** of truth: the Lord's raiment "white as snow" at the Transfiguration (Mark 9:3) is the Divine in ultimates (*AE* §67).  
* **Hail:** Corresponds to **Falsity from Evil** destroying the church (*AC* §7553).  
  * *Mechanism:* Hail is ice that destroys crops. It represents falsified truths (hard, cold dogmas) used to attack the good of the church. "Great hail" (Rev 16\) signifies "direful and atrocious falsities, by which every truth of the Word, and thence of the church, is destroyed" (*AR* §714).

## **5\. The Biosphere: The Vegetable Kingdom**

In Swedenborg’s taxonomy, the vegetable kingdom corresponds to the **Understanding** and its growth: trees signify perceptions and knowledges (*AC* §103). Plants are rooted in the earth (the mind), drink water (truth), and grow towards the sun (love).

### **5.1 The Sacred Triad: Olive, Vine, Fig**

Swedenborg identifies a specific correspondence for the three primary agricultural trees of the Bible, representing the three discrete degrees of the church and the human mind: "'vineyards' the spiritual things of the church, 'fig trees' the natural things, 'olive yards' the celestial things" (*AC* §1069; see also §§217, 886).

| Tree | Degree | Correspondence | Fruit/Product |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Olive Tree** | Celestial | **The Good of Love** (to the Lord) | Oil (The holiness of Love) |
| **Vine** | Spiritual | **Spiritual Good** (Charity) | Wine (Spiritual Truth) |
| **Fig Tree** | Natural | **Natural Good** (Moral Life) | Figs (Good Works) |

* **The Olive:** Corresponds to the **Celestial Church**. The oil signifies the essence of love (Charity) which heals, softens, and burns as fuel for light.  
* **The Vine:** Corresponds to the **Spiritual Church**. It represents the intellectual side of faith. "I am the Vine" (John 15\) signifies the Lord as the source of all spiritual truth. The branches are individual minds receiving that truth.  
* **The Fig:** Corresponds to the **Natural Church**. It represents the external life of obedience and kindness.  
  * *The Barren Fig Tree:* Corresponds to the natural man with **knowledge but no good of life**—having leaves (knowledges) but no fruit (the goods of life) (*AE* §109). This state is "cursed" because faith without works is dead.

### **5.2 Trees of Structure and Perception**

* **Cedar (of Lebanon):** Corresponds to **Rational Truth** of a high order—"truth from good in the rational" (*AE* §175).  
  * *Context:* The cedar is tall, majestic, and enduring. It corresponds to the rational mind that organizes truths into a coherent, protective system. It represents the "intellectual" spiritual man.  
* **Oak:** Corresponds to **Natural Perception**: perceptions "such as are human from memory-knowledges, and from the first rational things thence derived" (*AC* §2144; see §§1442–1443).
  * *Context:* The oak is strong, knotty, and has low-hanging branches. It corresponds to the perception of the natural mind—strong and enduring, but grounded in the senses rather than high spiritual insight. When Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, is "buried … under the oak" (Gen 35:8), hereditary evil in the natural is expelled and "rejected forever" (*AC* §§4562–4563).  
* **Trees in General:** Correspond to **Perceptions** and **Knowledges** (*AC* §103). The root is the knowledge embedded in the memory; the trunk is the disposition of the mind; the branches are the reach of reasoning; the leaves are the specific truths; the fruit is the good of life.

### **5.3 Grasses, Grains, and Thorns**

* **Wheat:** Corresponds to the **Good of Love** in the internal will: "the good of love and charity" (*AC* §3941). It is the "staff of life," representing the essential nourishment of the soul (Celestial Good).  
* **Barley:** Corresponds to **the good of the exterior natural** (*AC* §7602). It is a grain of significant value but "lighter" than wheat, corresponding to a lower degree of good.  
* **Grass:** Corresponds to **Scientifics** (Knowledge) and the **Intelligence of the Natural Man** (*AC* §7571).  
  * *Green Grass:* Living knowledge that serves a use; "'green grass' signifies true knowledge (*scientificum*), which is living" (*AE* §507).
  * *Burnt Grass:* Scientific truth destroyed: "all true knowledge (*scientificum*) was destroyed by the cupidities" of evil loves (Rev 8:7; *AE* §507).  
* **Thorns and Briers:** Correspond to **Falsities of Evil** (*AR* §439).  
  * *Mechanism:* Thorns are defensive outgrowths that choke the plant. They correspond to the lusts of self-love and the false rationalizations that spring up to choke the "Word" (the seed) in the mind. The "Crown of Thorns" signifies the mockery and falsification of Divine Truth by the Jewish Church, which "perverted everything that was said respecting Him in the Word" (*AE* §577).  
* **Wormwood:** Corresponds to **infernal falsity** (*AR* §410). It makes the water bitter, and "bitter" signifies what is falsified (*AR* §411): the falsification of truth until it becomes repulsive and spiritually poisonous.

### **5.4 Gardens vs. Forests**

* **The Garden (Eden):** Corresponds to **Intelligence and Wisdom**; a "garden" signifies intelligence, and "Eden" love (*AC* §100).  
  * *Meaning:* A garden is nature ordered by human (or Divine) intent. It corresponds to the mind cultivated by Divine Truths. "Eden" represents the wisdom of the Most Ancient Church where love and wisdom were perfectly united.  
* **The Forest:** Corresponds to the **Natural Man** and its dense perceptions—"the memory-knowledge of the natural man" (*AC* §10644).  
  * *Meaning:* A forest is wild and uncultivated. It represents the natural mind before regeneration—full of knowledge (trees) but disordered and potentially dark. "The beast of the forest" corresponds to the affections of the unregenerate natural man.

## **6\. The Biosphere: The Zoological Kingdom**

Animals correspond to **Affections** (feelings/emotions). While plants represent the *growth* of thought, animals represent the *life* of the will. A gentle animal corresponds to a good affection; a fierce animal to an evil lust. Swedenborg asserts that in the spiritual world, a person's affections often project themselves visibly as animals: "the animals of the earth correspond in general to affection, mild and useful animals to good affections, fierce and useless ones to evil affections" (*HH* §110).

### **6.1 Beasts of the Field: The Good Affections**

* **The Lamb:** Corresponds to **Innocence** (*AE* §314).  
  * *Context:* The lamb is the central symbol of the Celestial Heaven. It signifies the "Good of Innocence"—the state of trusting entirely in the Lord, like a child. The "Lamb of God" signifies the Lord’s Divine Human in its supreme innocence and mercy.  
* **The Sheep:** Correspond to **Charity** (Mutual Love) (*AE* §9; *AC* §4169). "My sheep hear my voice" signifies those who are in the good of charity and thus perceive truth.  
* **The Cow/Ox:** Corresponds to **Natural Good** (*AC* §2180).  
  * *Context:* These are beasts of burden and labor. They correspond to the affections of the natural man—duty, obedience, and the good of usefulness in the external world.  
* **The Horse:** Corresponds to the **Intellect** or **Understanding of the Word**: "a horse" signifies the understanding, and his "rider" one who is intelligent (*WH* §2).  
  * *Mechanism:* The horse carries the rider, just as the understanding carries the spirit.  
  * *White Horse:* Pure understanding of truth from the Word (Rev 19) (*AR* §820).
  * *Red Horse:* Understanding destroyed by evil (violence to the good) (*AR* §305).
  * *Black Horse:* Understanding destroyed by falsity (darkness) (*AR* §312).
  * *Pale Horse:* Spiritual death (total destruction of good and truth) (*AR* §320).
  * *Pegasus:* An ancient correspondence for the understanding elevating toward spiritual truths (*WH* §4).

### **6.2 Beasts of the Forest: The Ambivalent and Evil**

* **The Lion:** Corresponds to the **Power of Truth** from good (*AC* §§6367, 6441).  
  * *Genuine Sense:* The "Lion of the Tribe of Judah." This represents the Lord in His omnipotence of Divine Truth fighting against the hells. The lion’s roar corresponds to the power of divine revelation.  
  * *Opposite Sense:* The **Power of Falsity**. The "roaring lion" seeking to devour corresponds to the lust of self-love destroying the truths of the church: "the evil of the love of self in its power" (*AC* §6367).  
* **The Bear:** Corresponds to the **Natural Man** and the **Literal Sense of the Word**.  
  * *Genuine Sense:* Power in the ultimate/literal sense; "bears" signify truth in ultimates (*AE* §66). The bear is strong and shaggy.
  * *Opposite Sense:* Those who read the Word only literally, without understanding its spirit. This leads to fallacies and "bear-like" clumsiness in spiritual matters. The bear's feet in Rev 13 signify a heresy "full of fallacies from the sense of the letter of the Word read but not understood" (*AR* §573).  
* **The Wolf:** Corresponds to **Avarice** and the **Lust of Destroying Truths**: "one who seizes and scatters," "the avidity of seizing" (*AC* §6441).
  * *Meaning:* The wolf is a predator of sheep (charity). It signifies those who are inwardly deceitful, appearing innocent (sheep's clothing) but harboring hatred for the good of the church.  
* **The Leopard:** Corresponds to **Reasoning from Falsities** that appear like Truth: "a heresy destructive of the church, because it is from the truths of the Word falsified" (*AR* §572).  
  * *Mechanism:* The leopard has beautiful spots (truths) but is a ferocious beast. This corresponds to the doctrine of "Faith Alone," which uses scattered truths from the Bible to construct a system that destroys the life of charity. It represents a "patchwork" of heresy that looks intellectually impressive but is internally predatory.

### **6.3 Birds of the Air: Thoughts and Rationality**

Birds fly in the air (the atmosphere of thought). They correspond to **Rational Concepts**, **Intellectual Ideas**, and **Reasonings**—"things of understanding, of reason, and of memory-knowledge, and also the opposite, which are reasonings and falsities" (*AC* §866; see §40).

* **The Eagle:** Corresponds to **Rational Intelligence** and **Acute Perception**.  
  * *Context:* The eagle flies highest and has the keenest sight. It corresponds to the rational mind’s ability to see truths from a universal perspective (*AC* §3901). In the opposite sense, an eagle "signifies rational things that are not true, and thus are false" (*AC* §3901).  
* *The Dove:* Corresponds to the **Goods and Truths of Faith** and **Regeneration**: "the truths and goods of faith with him who is to be regenerated" (*AC* §870).  
  * *Context:* The dove is associated with the Holy Spirit. It signifies the gentle, purifying thoughts associated with regeneration and the innocence of faith.  
* *Birds of Night (Owls, Bats):* Correspond to **Falsities** and the darkness of the mind.  
  * *Context:* These creatures see in the dark (falsity) and are blinded by the light (truth); "owls, moles, birds of night, and bats … see nothing in the light of day" (*AE* §410; see *AC* §866). They correspond to the state of those in "self-intelligence" who reject Divine Truth and prefer the darkness of their own conceits.

### **6.4 Entomology and Reptiles: The Sensual Degree**

These creatures correspond to the **Sensual Degree** of the mind—the lowest level that interacts with the physical senses.

* **The Serpent:** Corresponds to the **Sensual Man** (*AC* §§195–197).  
  * *Genuine Sense:* **Prudence** and Circumspection. The sensual degree is necessary for life in the world ("Be ye wise as serpents").  
  * *Opposite Sense:* **Deceit** and reasoning from the senses. The serpent in Eden represents the "fallacy of the senses" which seduces the rational mind (Eve) into believing that life comes from nature, not God. The serpent crawling on the dust signifies the sensual mind groveling in corporeal things.  
* **The Dragon:** Corresponds to the **Profanation of Truth** and the **Separation of Faith from Charity**.  
  * *Context:* The Dragon (a great serpent) represents the intellectual monster created when knowledge of God is used to justify evil. It specifically signifies the theology of "Faith Alone" which persecutes the "Woman" (the True Church): those who "separate charity from faith" (*AR* §537).  
* **The Locust:** Corresponds to **Sensual Falsity** in the extremes (*AC* §7643).  
  * *Mechanism:* Locusts consume the green things (living truths) of the mind. They represent the appetite of the sensual man that consumes all spiritual understanding, leaving the mind barren (a spiritual famine).  
* **The Bee:** Corresponds to the **reasonings of the rational** gathering from the knowledges of the natural man.
  * *Context:* The bee collects nectar from flowers to make honey. "As bees suck out and derive their store from flowers, so the rational does from the knowledges of the natural man"; the bee of Assyria (Isa 7:18) is false reasoning so derived (*AE* §410). Honey signifies the delight of the natural (*AC* §5620).  
* **The Spider:** Corresponds to **Cunning and Deceit**: "their deceitful falsities are signified by 'the spider’s webs which they weave'" (*AE* §581).  
  * *Mechanism:* The spider weaves a web to entrap. This corresponds to the false doctrines spun from self-intelligence to trap others. The spider sits in the center of its web, just as the evil love of self sits in the center of its false reasonings; Swedenborg saw evil spirits station their emissaries "like a spider setting its web, the senders being in the middle" (*AC* §5984).  
* **The Worm:** Corresponds to **Infernal Torment** from falsity.
  * *Context:* "The torment in hell from the falsity is compared to 'a worm'; and the torment from the evil there is compared to 'fire'"—hence "their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched" (Isa 66:24; *AC* §8481). It represents the state of the damned who are consumed by their own falsities and lusts.

## **7\. The Chronosphere: Time, Space, and State**

In Swedenborgian physics, the "dimensions" of the natural world correspond to the "states" of the spiritual world. Time corresponds to **State of Life**, and Space corresponds to **State of Love**: in angelic thought, "instead of spaces such things as have reference to states of love, and instead of times such things as have reference to states of wisdom" (*DLW* §70); "all times signify states of life" (*AE* §23).

### **7.1 The Quarters of the Compass**

Directions are determined by the presence of the Lord (the Spiritual Sun) (*HH* §§141, 148).

* **East:** Corresponds to **Love to the Lord** (Celestial Love). The Lord is always in the East in the spiritual world.  
* **West:** Corresponds to love in obscure perception (*HH* §148), and so to a **declining state of love**—the third church was "like evening, autumn, and the west" (*TCR* §762)—and, in the hells, where the quarters are reversed, to the love of self (*HH* §587).  
* **South:** Corresponds to **Wisdom and Intelligence** (Light).  
* **North:** Corresponds to **Ignorance** or the obscurity of truth (Cold/Darkness).

### **7.2 The Seasonal Cycle**

The progression of the seasons corresponds to the progression of regeneration in the individual (*AC* §935) and the cycles of the Church in history: the most ancient church "was like morning, spring, and the east," the ancient "like day, summer, and the south," the third "like evening, autumn, and the west," and the fourth "like night, winter and the north" (*TCR* §762).

| Season | Spiritual Correspondence | State of the Church/Mind |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Spring** | New Birth / Peace | The first state of a new Church; Infancy; The beginning of regeneration where truth is first received with joy. |
| **Summer** | Fullness / Wisdom | The state of greatest light (wisdom) and heat (love); Mature Faith and Charity. |
| **Autumn** | Decline / Obscurity | The waning of love and faith; reliance on external memory; the "Evening" of the church. |
| **Winter** | End / Consummation | The total absence of love and truth; spiritual death; the state of selfhood without the Lord. |

### **7.3 The Colors of Creation**

Colors are the "modifications" of light and heat, corresponding to the variations of wisdom and love.

* **White:** Corresponds to **Truth** (reflecting light). It represents the purity of the spiritual degree (*AC* §3301).
* **Red:** Corresponds to **Good** (reflecting fire/love). It represents the Celestial degree (*AC* §3300).
* **Blue:** Corresponds to the **Celestial Love of Truth** (*AC* §9466).
* **Green:** Corresponds to **truth obscured** (*AE* §269); in grass it is living knowledge, the *scientificum* of the Natural degree (*AE* §507).
* **Purple:** Corresponds to the **Celestial Love of Good**, "by a red color is signified the good of celestial love" (*AC* §9467), signifying the Lord’s sovereignty through Love.

## **8\. Conclusion: The Living Mirror**

The cartography presented here reveals that, in the theology of Emanuel Swedenborg, the natural world is a language. It is not a mute collection of atoms but a living mirror where the spiritual drama of the universe is played out in fixed forms.

* The **Mineral Kingdom** provides the foundation, corresponding to the fixed truths and loves that ground our existence.  
* The **Vegetable Kingdom** displays the growth of the mind, from the first "grass" of scientific fact to the majestic "cedar" of rational understanding and the fruit-bearing "olive" of celestial love.  
* The **Animal Kingdom** reveals the spectrum of the heart, from the innocent "lamb" and the "sheep" of charity to the devouring "wolf" of avarice and the "dragon" of profaned religion.  
* The **Elements** themselves—sun, wind, and water—narrate the physics of the Divine presence, describing how Love (heat) and Wisdom (light) flow through the atmospheres to sustain creation.

This map demonstrates that there is no separation between the physical and the spiritual. The "World of Effects" is the "World of Causes" made visible. To walk through the natural world, in this view, is to walk through a library of Divine Wisdom, where every stone, leaf, and cloud invites the human mind to look inward and upward toward its Source.
