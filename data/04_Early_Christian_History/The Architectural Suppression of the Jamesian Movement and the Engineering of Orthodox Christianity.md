## **The Constantinian Shift: Financial Imperialism and State-Enforced Dogma**

The defining historical pivot in the evolution of Christianity occurred with the Constantinian Shift, a process that fundamentally merged the ecclesiastical infrastructure with the administrative and military power of the Roman Empire.14 Prior to the early fourth century, Christians had faced severe, state-sponsored violence, culminating in the "Great Persecution" initiated by Emperor Diocletian and his deputy Galerius in 303 CE, which sought to eradicate the religion through church destruction, scripture incineration, and the mass execution of believers.17 However, the political landscape inverted drastically following Constantine’s victory at the Battle of the Milvian Bridge in 312 CE and the subsequent issuance of the Edict of Milan in 313 CE, which legalized Christianity and restored its confiscated property.14
