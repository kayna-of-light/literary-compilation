# Source 012 — Carroll's texts quoted in the rewrite

**Editions**: *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland* (London: Macmillan, 1865; Project Gutenberg 11); *Through the Looking-Glass, and What Alice Found There* (London: Macmillan, 1871 [dated 1872]; Gutenberg 12); *Sylvie and Bruno* (1889; Gutenberg 620); *Sylvie and Bruno Concluded* (1893; Gutenberg 48795).
**Accessed**: 2026-10-23; each quotation found verbatim.

| Passage | Text | Locus |
|---|---|---|

Added 2026-10-23, each found verbatim: *Wonderland* ch. 1 ("the loveliest garden you ever saw"), ch. 7 ("Now, I'll manage better this time"; "He's murdering the time!"), ch. 8 ("the pattern on their backs was the same as the rest of the pack"), ch. 9 ("The Queen never executes nobody"), ch. 12 ("Sentence first—verdict afterwards"; "she had grown to her full size"); *Looking-Glass* ch. 1 ("only the things go the other way"; "what could be seen from the old room was quite common"), ch. 2 (the hill; the chessboard), ch. 5 (the King's Messenger; "I *had* done the things I was punished for"), ch. 8 ("Whenever the horse stopped … he fell off in front"; the upside-down box); *Sylvie and Bruno* (the Outlandish Watch undone: "the wounded youth was once more reclining on the heap of pillows"); *Sylvie and Bruno Concluded* ch. 4 ("*He* crowded it most"), ch. 24 ("SYLVIE WILL LOVE ALL" / "ALL WILL LOVE SYLVIE"). Chapter numbers for *Sylvie and Bruno* were not verified against the text and are therefore not used in the documents (the passages are cited by book only).
