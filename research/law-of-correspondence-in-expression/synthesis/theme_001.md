# Theme 001 — The chain, link by link

Each link is given with its evidence (store numbers) and its strength:

- **Documented**: stated in a primary or archival source.
- **Strong inference**: two documented facts that close the link between them.
- **Reading**: the framework's interpretation of documented facts.

## What the chain shows

The studio's method has a documented ancestry, and it is not the Theosophical atmosphere the library had assumed. It is the expression tradition. That tradition was taught in English, in its standard textbook, *as Swedenborg's doctrine of correspondence applied to the body*. It entered Russia through that textbook, was used by Stanislavsky, and reached the 1937 class through *An Actor Prepares*.

## What the chain does not show

- That Delsarte took his law from Swedenborg. Waille rejects it.
- That Graham or any 1937 animator read Stebbins, Delsarte or Swedenborg.
- That Stanislavsky knew of the Swedenborgian reading. Volkonsky set the religion and philosophy aside, and the name stays in one sentence of his book.

## Answer to the author's question

The missing middle is the American expositors' layer and its passage through Volkonsky to Stanislavsky. The 1937 class reached the law by the documented route the author suspected: a teaching that had been read through Swedenborg before it travelled, and that carried his order of inner and outer without his name.
