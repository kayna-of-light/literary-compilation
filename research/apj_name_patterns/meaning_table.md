# Meaning table: the members and forces of the Apocryphon's anthropogony, coded from Swedenborg

**Status**: Draft for the author's review (2026-11-09). Nothing is computed until this table is approved. It is the only place where assumptions enter the study.

**Rule of construction.** Every member and force named in the angel lists of the Apocryphon of John (NHC II 15,13–19,2; IV 24–29) receives its spiritual counterpart in one of two ways:

- **S (stated)**: Swedenborg states the correspondence of this member or of its function. The section is cited and quoted.
- **D (derived)**: Swedenborg does not treat the member. Its counterpart is derived from the member's natural function, as he derives his own: "the heavens … correspond chiefly to the functions of these viscera or organs … the use existed before the organic forms of the body came forth; and … the use produced and adapted them to itself" (*AC* §4223). The derivation is stated in the row, so that it can be judged.

**The table contains no angel names.** The meanings are coded from the members alone. The names are attached only when the analysis is run, so that the coding cannot be shaped, even unawares, by how a name sounds.

## 1. The coordinates

Each member is described by three coordinates. Only the first two vary in content; the third is a fixed set of components.

### 1.1 Region (degree)

| Code | Region | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | Head, down to the neck | "the highest or third heaven forms the head down to the neck" (*HH* §65); "celestial things constitute the head" (*AC* §4938) |
| **B** | Breast down to the loins and knees | "the middle or second heaven forms the breast down to the loins and knees" (*HH* §65); "spiritual [things constitute] the body" (*AC* §4938) |
| **A** | Arms down to the fingers | "the lowest or first heaven forms … also the arms down to the fingers" (*HH* §65) |
| **F** | Feet down to the soles | "the lowest or first heaven forms the feet down to the soles" (*HH* §65); "natural [things constitute] the feet" (*AC* §4938) |

### 1.2 Side

| Code | Side | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | Right | "those things which are on the right side of man correspond to the good from which are truths" (*AC* §10061) |
| **L** | Left | "those which are on the left correspond to the truths through which is good" (*AC* §10061); the same of the brain (*AC* §4052) and of the face and eyes (*AC* §4326) |
| **–** | Unpaired | — |

### 1.3 Components

Each member's meaning is broken into one or more of thirteen components, so that different members can share a meaning. The components are taken from the distinctions Swedenborg himself draws across the Grand Man chapters; none is introduced for this study.

| Component | Meaning | Defined from |
|---|---|---|
| **GOOD** | Love, will, the celestial | heart and its kingdom (*AC* §§3887–3889); heat as love (*HH* §§133–136); flesh as the own of the will (*AC* §3813) |
| **TRUTH** | Understanding, faith, the spiritual | lungs and respiration (*AC* §3888); sight as understanding (*AC* §4406); the reins as truth (*AC* §5385); the tongue as the affection of truth (*AC* §4791) |
| **PERCEPTION** | Reception through a sense | the five senses and their affections (*AC* §4404); the nostrils as "perceptions" (*AC* §4624); hearing (*AC* §4653) |
| **POWER** | Power, strength | "the hands, arms, and shoulders correspond to power" (*AC* §4933) |
| **PURIFICATION** | Separating the useful from the useless; exploring quality | stomach (*AC* §5174), liver (*AC* §5183), kidneys (*AC* §5381), intestines (*AC* §5392) |
| **THRESHOLD** | Admitting or rejecting what enters; guard of an entrance | skins as "entrances or thresholds to heaven … they either reject or admit" (*AC* §5553); the liver "as a guard before the heart" (*AC* §5183); the stomach as the place where spirits are "not in the Grand Man, but are being introduced into it" (*AC* §5176) |
| **NOURISHMENT** | Supplying food to the blood and heart | the tongue "ministers to the stomach, which supplies food to the blood and heart" (*AC* §4791); chyle into blood (*AC* §5174) |
| **CIRCULATION** | Carrying life through the whole body | the heart's pulse and the lungs' respiration in the Grand Man (*AC* §3884); juices "imbibed by the veins and carried into the circulation" (*AC* §5174) |
| **CONJUNCTION** | Joining, binding together | the knees as "the conjunction of the truth of faith with the good of love" (*AC* §3915); "the union between the heart and the lungs … the marriage of good and truth" (*AC* §3889) |
| **CONJUGIAL** | Conjugial love; generation | "the loins and the members adhering to them correspond to genuine conjugial love" (*AC* §5050); thigh (*AC* §3021) |
| **SUPPORT** | Bearing and holding together; little life | "the cartilages and bones which support and hold together all the parts of the body … have little spiritual life" (*AC* §§5552, 5560) |
| **INTEGUMENT** | External covering, reacting against what is within | the skin, which "reacts against internals" (*AC* §5554); the dura and pia mater as integuments of the brain (*AC* §§4046–4047); hair (*AC* §5569) |
| **NATURAL** | The natural, the lowest | "by 'feet' … natural things, by 'soles of the feet' lower natural things, and by 'heels' the lowest natural things" (*AC* §4938); "the beard and teeth signify the lowest natural things" (*AC* §5387) |

## 4. Points for the author's decision

1. **The thirteen components.** Are these the right distinctions? Should any be merged (for example THRESHOLD into PURIFICATION) or split (for example TRUTH into the affection of truth and obedience)?
2. **The derived rows** (D): tonsils, uvula, neck, fingers, belly, navel, hypochondrium, marrow, spleen, sinews, arteries, shins, toes, toenails, bosom, and several forces. Each derivation is written out; any that you would derive differently should be changed now.
3. **Members with more than one reading.** The breast as charity (*AC* §4403) and as nourishment (from function); the knee as conjunction and as conjugial love (*AC* §§3915, 3021); the brain as both good and truth. These are coded with both components.
4. **Rows resting on sources not rechecked**: the nails and sinews (*Spiritual Diary*, as cited in *The Unnamed Cartography*).
