# Experience and Literature: research plan

**Started**: 2026-11-03, at the author's request, after *From Planets to Constellations* (`data/02_Swedenborgian_Theology/`) was rebuilt on two layers (PR #63, merged).

## Why this project

The rebuilt document spends most of its weight exposing the contact and channelled literature. The author's point is a different one. Beneath the literature lies honest experience, and the argument has to say what that experience looks like and how it differs from the literature built on top of it. The argument must stand on published sources alone. The author is the researcher here, so her own experiences cannot serve as evidence for it.

## The questions

Each question is answered only from published material.

1. **Spontaneous versus produced.** Do published studies of spontaneous experiences (NDEs, spontaneous out-of-body experiences, unbidden visions) differ from channelled and séance material in content and structure: cosmology, names and coordinates, how much stays unexplained? The distinction is not whether the experiencer was looking for understanding, but whether the experience is produced on demand.
2. **Uncertainty.** Do experiencers in the published record keep their own uncertainty, and do interpretive labels attach to their accounts later?
3. **Direction of change.** What does published research on aftereffects show: more humility and less self-focus, or a sense of being special? How does that compare with the self-exalting claims in channelled texts?
4. **Stars as signs.** How does Swedenborg read stars: as signs of heavenly realities, or as addresses? How does the library's literalization thesis (a correspondential sign read as a literal place) apply to the move from planets to stars?
5. **Experience pointing to checkable things.** Are there documented, published cases where a spontaneous experience led to something verifiable? How do checks of channelled factual claims and predictions turn out?

## Rules for this project

- No personal experience of the author is used, quoted or alluded to.
- Every claim in the eventual document traces to a published source listed in `store/`.
- Report what each study measured, in what sample, by what method. Do not recruit objections, and do not hide findings that cut against the argument.
- No pirated copies.

## Outputs

- `context.md`: what the library already holds, organized by topic.
- `store/source_NNN.md`: one file per external source.
- `synthesis/`: themes across sources.
- Then a report to the author before *From Planets to Constellations* is changed.
