# Source 012 — Findings that complicate a reading of mediumship as fabrication

Kept so that the argument does not recruit objections in reverse: it must not overstate the case against channelling either.

**Citations** (reading status per item):
- Elizabeth C. Roxburgh and Chris A. Roe, "A Survey of Dissociation, Boundary-Thinness, and Psychological Wellbeing in Spiritualist Mental Mediumship," *Journal of Parapsychology* 75, no. 2 (2011): 279–299. https://nectar.northampton.ac.uk/id/eprint/3903/. Abstract read by the agent.
- Julio F. Peres, Alexander Moreira-Almeida, Leonardo Caixeta, Frederico Leao and Andrew Newberg, "Neuroimaging during Trance State: A Contribution to the Study of Dissociation," *PLoS ONE* 7, no. 11 (2012): e49360. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0049360. Read by the agent.

- Julie Beischel and Gary E. Schwartz, "Anomalous Information Reception by Research Mediums Demonstrated Using a Novel Triple-Blind Protocol," *Explore* 3, no. 1 (2007): 23–27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.explore.2006.10.004. Abstract read by the agent.

None of these has been read myself. All must be checked before any is cited.

## What they report

- **Roxburgh & Roe.** 159 Spiritualists (80 mediums, 79 non-mediums). "No significant differences were found between the groups on dissociation or boundary-thinness." Mediums reported higher wellbeing and lower distress.
- **Peres et al.** 10 Brazilian psychographers, scanned with SPECT. Experienced writers showed lower activity in several regions during trance writing. A blinded rater scored the trance texts as more complex than control writing. The authors: "not consistent with faking or role-playing." They state their own limits: small sample, no correction for multiple comparisons.
- **Rocha et al.** 13 Chico Xavier letters attributed to one deceased man. Of 99 verifiable items, about 98% were rated a "Clear and Precise Fit." Fraud, chance, leakage and cold reading were judged "only remotely plausible."
- **Beischel & Schwartz.** 8 mediums under a triple-blind design: intended readings rated higher than controls (p = 0.007). The authors note the design "cannot distinguish among alternative paranormal hypotheses."
- **O'Keeffe & Wiseman.** "The results of this work did not support the existence of genuine mediumistic ability."

## Why it matters

The two-layer argument does not need channelling to be fraud, and should not claim it.
- Swedenborg's own position is that speech with spirits is real and dangerous: spirits "say almost anything" and "lie" (*Spiritual Diary* §1622), and they "see falsities as truths" (*Heaven and Hell* §249). That is a claim about the unreliability of the content, not about whether contact occurs.
- Flournoy, the most careful early investigator, also found "a little telekinesis and telepathy."

The defensible line:
- **Production.** Channelled material is produced in a willed mode (source 006). It is shaped by the channel's library and era (source 011), and organized around exalting the receiver (sources 007, 008).
- **Content.** Its cosmological content, the star addresses included, carries no weight as information. That holds whatever share of mediumistic contact may be real.
