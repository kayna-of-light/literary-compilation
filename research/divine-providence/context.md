### The Greek Text (Rev 12:1-6, 13-17)

The woman clothed with the sun (divine love as garment), moon under her feet (faith as foundation), crown of twelve stars (complete truths of wisdom). She is pregnant and in labor. The dragon stands before her to devour the child. The child is caught up to God. The woman flees into the wilderness — τόπον ἡτοιμασμένον ἀπὸ τοῦ θεοῦ — "a place prepared from God." She is nourished there for 1,260 days — the same 42 months the beast rules the surface. They are bilateral states of the same period.

The dragon, unable to reach her, casts water like a river (falsity in a flood) after her. The earth helps her — swallows the river. The dragon, enraged, goes to make war with "the rest of her seed" — those keeping the commandments and holding the testimony. Chapter 13 (the beasts) is the dragon's answer to its failure in chapter 12.

- The water as a river = falsity in a flood to sweep the woman away
- The earth swallowing the river = the natural plane absorbing/neutralizing the flood of falsity
