## Research Question

Produce a comprehensive scholarly inventory of all texts contained in the Nag Hammadi Library (NHC I–XIII), including the Berlin Gnostic Codex (BG 8502) and the Codex Tchacos where relevant for comparison.

For each tractate, provide:

1. **Codex number and position** (e.g., NHC II,2)
2. **Title** (as given in the manuscript, with scholarly English translation)
3. **Language and script** (Sahidic, Subachmimic, etc.)
4. **Estimated date of composition** (scholarly consensus range, citing the principal scholars who established the dating)
5. **Genre classification** (revelation dialogue, gospel, apocalypse, treatise, prayer, letter, wisdom collection, philosophical excerpt, etc.)
6. **Cosmological features**: Does the text contain any of the following? (yes/no with brief note)
   - A named Demiurge (Yaldabaoth, Saklas, Samael, or equivalent)
   - A Sophia myth (fall, emanation, error)
   - Archon cosmology (planetary rulers, toll-houses)
   - Creation of the material world as error or imprisonment
7. **Named protagonists or attributed authorities** (to whom is the teaching attributed within the text?)
8. **Scholarly classification**: How have major scholars classified this text? (e.g., Turner's "Sethian," Layton's "Classic Gnostic," Thomassine, Valentinian, Hermetic, Jewish-Christian wisdom, philosophical, unclassified)

Present the results in a single table with all columns above. Follow the table with a brief summary noting:
- How many texts contain full Demiurgical cosmology
- How many lack it entirely
- How many fall in an intermediate category (e.g., reference to "rulers" or "authorities" without developed Demiurgical mythology)

## Output Requirements

- Cite scholarly editions and translations (Robinson, Layton, Meyer, Marvin Meyer, etc.)
- Use peer-reviewed sources for dating and classification
- Do not impose a single overarching category on the collection — report what individual scholars have said about individual texts
- Where scholarly opinion is divided on classification, note the disagreement

## Exclusions

- Do not provide theological interpretation of the texts
- Do not evaluate the collection as a whole — this query concerns individual tractates only
