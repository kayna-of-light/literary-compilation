## Research Question

The Gospel of Philip (NHC II,3) contains several passages that mention Mary Magdalene and describe her relationship to Jesus in terms that have generated significant scholarly discussion. This query requests a thorough philological and text-critical analysis of these passages.

### A. Text and Translation

For every passage in the Gospel of Philip that mentions Mary Magdalene (or a "Mary" plausibly identified as Magdalene by scholars), provide:

1. The Coptic text (transliterated)
2. A literal English translation
3. The location in the manuscript (page and line numbers in the Facsimile Edition)
4. The condition of the manuscript at that point (lacunae, damage, reconstruction)
5. Major variant translations proposed by scholars (e.g., Isenberg, Layton, Schenke, Turner)

### B. Key Terms

For each relevant passage, analyze the following terms where they appear:

1. **κοινωνός (koinōnos)**: What is the semantic range of this term in Coptic, Greek, and contemporaneous Christian literature? How have scholars translated it in this specific context? (companion, consort, partner, etc.) What are the arguments for and against each translation?
2. **ⲙⲁⲣⲓϩⲁⲙ / Mariham / Mariamne / Mariamene**: What name form is actually used in the Coptic manuscript? How does this compare to other early Christian texts' designation of Mary Magdalene? Is it distinguishable from references to other Marys (mother of Jesus, Mary of Bethany)?
3. **The "kiss" passage**: What does the text actually say? (Account for lacunae.) What is the scholarly reconstruction? What parallel practices (liturgical kiss, kiss of peace) provide context?

### C. The "Three Marys" Passage

The Gospel of Philip contains a passage listing "three who always walked with the Lord: Mary, his mother, and her sister, and Magdalene, the one who was called his companion" (or similar, depending on translation).

1. Provide the full text and translation
2. What scholarly discussion exists regarding the syntax and referents?
3. How does this passage relate to the lists of women in the canonical Gospels (Mark 15:40, John 19:25)?

### D. Scholarly Interpretations

Summarize the major scholarly positions on what the Philip passages indicate about the relationship between Jesus and Mary Magdalene:

1. Scholars who argue the language indicates a marital or conjugal relationship, and their evidence
2. Scholars who argue the language is purely spiritual/sacramental (related to the "Bridal Chamber" ritual), and their evidence
3. Scholars who remain agnostic or note the ambiguity

### E. The Bridal Chamber Sacrament

The Gospel of Philip describes a sacrament called the "Bridal Chamber" (νυμφών / ⲡⲙⲁ ⲛϣⲉⲗⲉⲉⲧ).

1. What does the text say about this sacrament?
2. Is it connected to the Marian passages, or are these separate theological concerns?
3. What scholarly interpretations exist? (Valentinian ritual, metaphor for spiritual union, echo of Temple theology, etc.)
4. What parallels exist in other early Christian or Jewish texts?

## Output Requirements

- Base all analysis on the Coptic text as published in the critical editions
- Distinguish clearly between what the manuscript says, what scholars reconstruct in lacunae, and what scholars interpret
- Present the full range of scholarly opinion without favoring one position
- Cite peer-reviewed publications: critical editions (Layton, Isenberg, Schenke), monographs (DeConick, Marjanen, Pagels), and journal articles
- Do not cite popular or devotional sources
