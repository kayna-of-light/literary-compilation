## 1. Literalization: a sign read as a place

- **The mechanism.** When a correspondential text is received without the key, "dynamic states become static entities … The interior becomes exterior. The quality becomes a person. The process becomes an event" (*Reversing the Arrow* §1).
- **The heavens become locations.** *Zostrianos* keeps its correspondences, "but the heavens have been spatialized into locations" (*Reversing the Arrow* §5.1). The Pleroma "gets geography (numbered heavens, specific populations, administrative hierarchies)" (§10.1). The mountain on which the words are kept "is not a location. It is a state" (§3.1).
- **Origin stories as filters.** In the Apocalypse of Adam, each of the thirteen kingdoms gives the Illuminator an origin story through its own categories. "The generation without a king" gives none: "They give no birth story, because they do not NEED to explain him" (*Reversing the Arrow* §§4.1–4.2). The library reads this as "constant state, variable form" stated as narrative (§4.1).
- **Bearing on this project.** The stellar addresses of the contact literature are a modern case of the same move: a quality is given a place of origin. The library already holds the general thesis; this project would add the modern case and the evidence from experience.

## 2. Swedenborg's own anchoring

- **The mechanism in Swedenborg.** "The compulsion to anchor continuous spiritual processes in discrete physical explanations" (*The Selfhood of the Prophet* §3.6). Four instances: the limbus, biological Christology, uniform canonicity, and the date 1757.
- **Private uncertainty becomes public certainty.** For 1757, the *Diary* says "what they signify I do not clearly know," and the published *Last Judgment* gives a date (*Selfhood of the Prophet* §3.5.4, citing *SD* §§2026–2033, 2973–2978).
- **The planets are not among the four instances.** This project's source 001 adds a spatial instance with the same shape: in *SD* §1670, "I am not sure"; in *EU* §§148–149, an earth in the starry heaven.
- **Stars in his key.** Stars signify "knowledges of good and truth" (*A Library of Biblical Correspondences*, stars entry; verified in the primary text, source 002).

## 3. Reception and claiming: the direction criterion

- **The distinction.** "The receiving posture is 'the Lord in me' — reception that knows itself as reception. The claiming posture is 'I am the source'" (*The Empty Room* §2.4).
- **The driver test.** A system "moved by love" terminates in an Other who is loved; one "moved by self-love" terminates in the self's own elevation (*The Empty Room* §2.3).
- **The lineage.** *The Empty Room* §14 traces Theosophy through Alice Bailey, the "I AM" Activity and the Church Universal and Triumphant to the New Age "channeled entities" and the "starseed" literature, "in which advanced souls are said to incarnate from elsewhere to help humanity awaken to its innate godhood." It names six recurring axioms: an impersonal divine; the self as divine spark; ascent by one's own effort; hidden Masters or higher beings; a lost wisdom "held in the East, in Atlantis, in the stars"; service as a vehicle of self-ascent.
  - Caution: §14 states this lineage as "documented intellectual history" but gives no scholarly citation for the steps. External sources are needed (thread 7: Hanegraaff 1996; Brown 1997; Melton).
- **Filling the gap.** "Never back-fill an undecoded gap with a flattering source" (*The Empty Room* §17). Swedenborg labeled the gap ("seek for it in China; I have not got it"); Blavatsky filled it with herself. The same contrast applies to experiencers who leave an experience unexplained, against literature that supplies an origin.

## 4. An issue to raise with the author

*The Empty Room* §16, "The Experiential Falsification," uses a single anonymized first-person account as evidence: a child's recurring hostile entity, ended by an appeal to the Lord, presented as a "black swan" that falsifies the Theosophical axioms. On 2026-11-03 the author stated that using the researcher's own experiences to prove an argument is biased. Whose account §16 reports is not stated in the document. This is flagged to the author, not changed.

## 5. NDE phenomenology (context only)

*A Phenomenological Framework of the NDE* (unaudited) describes beings by function and presence: relatives, a Being of Light "often described in more general terms as a being of pure love and intelligence," and unidentified "guide" figures, with telepathic communication (Part II §5). It describes environments (light, landscapes, "cities of light," formless states) without stellar addresses (Part I §4). These descriptions are not to be cited as evidence. Published studies (threads 6, 8, 9) and the audited NDE notebooks are the evidence.
