## Background Context

The Correspondential Hypothesis interprets the 32 geometric signs as a spiritual vocabulary. Within this framework:

- **Tectiforms** (house/hut shapes) = "Houses of the Mind" — structured doctrine, organized knowledge, mental architecture providing shelter
- **Crosshatch/Grid** = "Net of Order" — containment of chaos, structured organization
- **Claviforms** (key/club shapes) = Authority, power to open or close, potentially weaponized truth

In Swedenborgian theology, "vastation" refers to periods of spiritual crisis when old forms fail and new containers must be built. The "ark" in Noah is the paradigmatic image — when the flood comes, you build a structure to preserve what is essential.

### The Prediction

> Periods of climatic or social stress (loss of "Good") should see a spike in signs corresponding to "protection" (Tectiforms) and "order" (Crosshatch/Grid), representing the need to build "arks" for the mind.

### Initial Evidence

- **Tectiforms cluster in the Magdalenian** (17,000-10,000 BP) — this period follows the Last Glacial Maximum (LGM), a time of intense climatic stress, megafauna extinction, and social reorganization
- Von Petzinger notes that Tectiforms are highly regional (Franco-Cantabrian) and temporally concentrated
- The Magdalenian is also characterized by peak artistic complexity and codification

---

## Research Question

**Primary Question**: Is there a correlation between periods of climatic/environmental stress in the Upper Paleolithic and the frequency or elaboration of "protective" geometric signs (Tectiforms, Crosshatch, Grids)?

### Specific Sub-Questions

1. **Temporal Distribution**: What is the temporal distribution of Tectiforms across the Upper Paleolithic? Do they cluster in specific periods?

2. **Climatic Correlation**: What were the major climatic events/stress periods during the Upper Paleolithic (LGM, Heinrich events, Younger Dryas)? Do peaks in certain sign types correlate with these events?

3. **Regional Specificity**: Why are Tectiforms concentrated in Franco-Cantabria? Was this region under particular stress during the Magdalenian? Or did it serve as a refugium (population aggregation)?

4. **Crosshatch/Grid Patterns**: What is the temporal distribution of crosshatch and grid motifs? Do they follow similar clustering patterns?

5. **Alternative Signs During Stress**: Did ANY sign types increase during stress periods? If not Tectiforms, what DID people mark during crises?

6. **Social Aggregation Evidence**: The Magdalenian shows evidence of larger social gatherings. Is there correlation between aggregation sites and sign density/complexity?

## What Would Confirm the Prediction

- **Statistical correlation** between Tectiform frequency and climatic stress periods (within dating resolution)
- **Qualitative pattern**: Tectiforms appearing in "aggregation" contexts where displaced populations gathered
- **Increase in "ordering" signs** (crosshatch, grid) during or immediately after major stress events
- **Scholars noting** the "doctrinal" or "codified" nature of late Magdalenian sign use

## What Would Falsify the Prediction

- **No temporal clustering** of Tectiforms — they appear randomly throughout the Upper Paleolithic
- **Inverse correlation** — Tectiforms appearing during stable, prosperous periods rather than stress periods
- **Sign frequency overall** decreasing during stress (less marking, not more "protective" marking)

## Critical Warnings

1. **DATING RESOLUTION**: Radiocarbon dating has error margins. Correlations at the century level may be spurious.
2. **DO NOT conflate** "Magdalenian = stressed" — it was a complex period with both crisis and cultural florescence
3. **CORRELATION ≠ CAUSATION**: Even if the pattern holds, the mechanism remains speculative
4. **REGIONAL BIAS**: Most cave art data is from Franco-Cantabria. "Clusters" may reflect research density, not ancient reality.
5. **DO NOT assume** that Tectiforms meant "shelter/protection" to the makers — that's the hypothesis, not the evidence
