# NHL Research Query 3: Pauline Presence and Absence in the Nag Hammadi Library

**Target System**: Gemini Deep Research  
**Domain**: Early Christian Literature / Nag Hammadi Studies  
**Status**: Open  
**Created**: 2026-02-13

---

## Research Question

The Pauline epistles constitute the earliest surviving Christian literary corpus and exerted decisive influence on the formation of the New Testament canon. This query investigates the degree to which the Nag Hammadi texts engage with, reflect, or ignore the Pauline tradition.

Conduct a systematic survey of the NHL corpus (NHC I–XIII, BG 8502) addressing:

### A. Explicit References to Paul

1. Which NHL texts mention Paul by name (including variant forms: Paulos, Saul)?
2. In what context is he mentioned? (positively, negatively, neutrally)
3. Is the *Prayer of the Apostle Paul* (NHC I,1) genuinely Pauline in theology, or does it merely bear his name? What do scholars say about its relationship to Pauline thought?

### B. Pauline Theological Concepts

For each of the following distinctively Pauline doctrines, identify which NHL texts (if any) engage with them, and whether positively, negatively, or in modified form:

- Justification by faith (δικαιοσύνη ἐκ πίστεως)
- The body of Christ (σῶμα Χριστοῦ) as ecclesiology
- Christ crucified as salvific event (σταυρός as locus of redemption)
- Resurrection of the body (σῶμα πνευματικόν)
- The collection/offering for Jerusalem
- Apostolic authority derived from vision/revelation rather than personal discipleship

### C. Anti-Pauline Polemic

Do any NHL texts contain what scholars have identified as implicit or explicit polemic against Pauline positions? Cite the relevant scholarly arguments (e.g., Pagels on anti-Pauline traditions; Lüdemann on Paul and the Jerusalem pillars).

### D. Comparative Context

For comparison: how prominent is Pauline theology in other roughly contemporaneous Christian collections or corpora? (e.g., the Apostolic Fathers, the Didache, the Pseudo-Clementines) This provides a baseline against which to assess Paul's presence or absence in the NHL.

## Output Requirements

- Distinguish between explicit reference and possible allusion — do not overread echoes
- Report negative findings (texts with no Pauline engagement) as data, not as absence of data
- Cite peer-reviewed scholarship throughout
- Where scholars disagree on whether a passage engages Pauline theology, present both positions
- Do not supply an interpretive framework for the findings — simply report what is and is not present
