# Source 003 — Collingwood, *The Life and Letters of Lewis Carroll* (1898); *Selected Letters*

**Citations**: Collingwood, Stuart Dodgson. *The Life and Letters of Lewis Carroll (Rev. C. L. Dodgson).* London: T. Fisher Unwin, 1898 (Project Gutenberg 11483). — Carroll, Lewis. *The Selected Letters of Lewis Carroll.* Edited by Morton N. Cohen. London: Macmillan, 1982 (archive.org `selectedletterso0000carr`, `selectedletterso00carr`).
**Accessed**: 2026-10-21/23.

## Findings
- Collingwood: "When he promised to write out 'Alice' for Miss Liddell he had no idea of publication; but his friend, Mr. George Macdonald, to whom he had shown the story, persuaded him to submit it to a publisher."
- Neither Collingwood nor the *Selected Letters* mentions Swedenborg (full-text search; both indexed — MacDonald found in each).
- Cohen and Green's full *Letters* (2 vols, Cambridge UP, 1979): the archive.org scan of vol. 1 (`dli.ernet.65641`) has no searchable text; not checked.

## Why it matters
Confirms MacDonald's part in the publication of *Alice* (with source_002, both versions — the children's reading and MacDonald's persuasion — are documented). Adds two more negative searches for Swedenborg in Carroll's own words.
