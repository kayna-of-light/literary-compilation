**Accessed**: 2026-10-24, archive.org full-text search, item `stanislavskysyst0000whym` (lending copy; read by phrase-probing, so the quotations are exact but page numbers were not captured).

## Why it matters

A Cambridge monograph on the System states that Stanislavsky used Delsarte's work, and it ties exercises in the System's line to Stebbins's book by page. This is independent of Autant-Mathieu (store 010) and agrees with her.
