## Key Findings From Primary Text Reading

### 1. "Man has no Body distinct from his Soul" (Marriage, Plate 4)

Blake's "Voice of the Devil" identifies three "Errors" of "All Bibles or sacred codes":
1. That Man has two real existing principles: a Body & a Soul
2. That Energy, call'd Evil, is alone from the Body; & that Reason, call'd Good, is alone from the Soul
3. That God will torment Man in Eternity for following his Energies

Blake's "Contraries to these":
1. Man has no Body distinct from his Soul; for that call'd Body is a portion of Soul discern'd by the five Senses, the chief inlets of Soul in this age
2. Energy is the only life and is from the Body, and Reason is the bound or outward circumference of Energy
3. Energy is Eternal Delight

**Analysis**: The first error Blake identifies IS the Cartesian dualism that the library identifies as Swedenborg's first distortion (the Limbus). Blake's correction — the body IS a portion of soul — IS the doctrine of correspondence stated without the dualistic separation Swedenborg introduces.

### 2. "The ancient Poets animated all sensible objects" (Marriage, Plate 11)

> "The ancient Poets animated all sensible objects with Gods or Geniuses, calling them by the names and adorning them with properties of woods, rivers, mountains, lakes, cities, nations, and whatever their enlarged & numerous senses could perceive."

> "Till a system was formed, which some took advantage of & enslav'd the vulgar by attempting to realize or abstract the mental deities from their objects: thus began Priesthood."

> "Choosing forms of worship from poetic tales."

> "Thus men forgot, that All deities reside in the human breast."

**Analysis**: Blake describes the EXACT trajectory documented in the Crucible thesis — from living perception ("animated all sensible objects") to systematic codification ("a system was formed") to institutional capture ("enslav'd the vulgar") to forgetting ("men forgot"). The error is ABSTRACTION — removing the spiritual content from the natural form and placing it in a separate location (temple, book, system). "Thus began Priesthood."

### 3. The Isaiah and Ezekiel Passage (Marriage, Plates 12-13)

Isaiah: "I saw no God, nor heard any, in a finite organical perception: but my senses discover'd the infinite in every thing."

Ezekiel: "The philosophy of the east taught the first principles of human perception, some nations held one principle for the origin & some another, we of Israel taught that the Poetic Genius (as you now call it) was the first principle and all the others merely derivative."

**Analysis**: Blake's Ezekiel describes "the philosophy of the east" — the Ancient Word — as teaching "the first principles of human perception." This IS the Alexandrian synthesis described in Blake's vocabulary: multiple nations receiving the same ancient tradition differently ("some nations held one principle... some another"). And Israel's specific claim was that "the Poetic Genius was the first principle" — the Lord as the source from which all perception derives.

### 4. "If the doors of perception were cleansed" (Marriage, Plate 14)

> "If the doors of perception were cleansed every thing would appear to man as it is: Infinite. For man has closed himself up, till he sees all things thro' narrow chinks of his cavern."

**Analysis**: The world is not corrupt. It APPEARS corrupt because perception is narrowed. Cleanse the perception and the SAME world appears infinite and holy. Not escape from the natural — transformation of the perceiver's relationship to it.

### 5. "A candle in sunshine" (Marriage, Plate 22)

Blake on Swedenborg: "Any man of mechanical talents may from the writings of Paracelsus or Jacob Behmen, produce ten thousand volumes of equal value with Swedenborgs, and from those of Dante or Shakespear, an infinite number. But when he has done this, let him not say that he knows better than his master, for he only holds a candle in sunshine."

**Analysis**: Swedenborg's systematic analysis is a candle — useful but not the light itself. The reality is SUNSHINE — the direct light of the Lord. The system is a tool, not the perception. Blake is not dismissing Swedenborg — he's placing the system in proper relation to the source.

### 6. The Book of Urizen — The Fall as Systematization

Urizen's declaration (Ch. II.4-8): "From the depths of dark solitude... I have sought for a joy without pain, / For a solid without fluctuation... Here alone I, in books form'd of metals, / Have written the secrets of Wisdom... One command, one joy, one desire, / One curse, one weight, one measure, / One King, one God, one Law."

**Analysis**: Urizen's sin is not malice. It is the desire for a SYSTEM — "a joy without pain, a solid without fluctuation." His laws are good in CONTENT ("peace, love, unity, pity, compassion, forgiveness") but tyrannical in FORM ("One command, one joy, one desire, one King, one God, one Law"). The imposition of uniformity IS the Fall. "One law for the Lion & Ox is Oppression."

### 7. The Net of Religion (Urizen, Ch. VIII-IX)

Urizen wanders his creation, sees its suffering, and weeps. His tears become "a cold Shadow... like a spider's web... Drawing out from his sorrowing soul." This web spreads wherever he walks. "The meshes, twisted like to the human brain. And all call'd it the Net of Religion."

Under the Net, perception contracts: "the shrunken eyes, clouded over, / Discern'd not the woven Hypocrisy." The Net becomes invisible. People think they see clearly while looking through the mesh.

"Six days they shrunk up from existence, / And on the seventh day they rested, / And they bless'd the seventh day, in sick hope, / And forgot their Eternal life."

**Analysis**: Religion is not the original perception. It is the rational mind's RESPONSE to the consequences of its own sovereignty — a web of management spun from pity over what its own systematization has broken. The Net makes itself invisible. The six days of creation become six days of CONTRACTION. The seventh day of rest becomes not divine satisfaction but exhausted resignation. "They forgot their Eternal life."

### 8. The Devil's Speech About Jesus (Marriage, Plate 23-24)

The Devil: "Jesus was all virtue, and acted from impulse: not from rules."

And: "The worship of God is, Honouring his gifts in other men each according to his genius, and loving the greatest men best, those who envy or calumniate great men hate God, for there is no other God."

**Analysis**: Jesus as a transparent vessel — acting from impulse (direct influx) not from rules (systematic law). God present IN human gifts, IN human genius. Not elsewhere. "There is no other God" than the God present in human beings.
