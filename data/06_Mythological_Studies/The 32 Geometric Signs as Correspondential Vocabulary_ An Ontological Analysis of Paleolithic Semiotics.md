# **The 32 Geometric Signs as Correspondential Vocabulary: An Ontological Analysis of Paleolithic Semiotics**

## **Executive Summary**

The persistence of geometric ornamentation in Upper Paleolithic cave art—spanning nearly 30,000 years and extending from the Iberian Peninsula to the Indonesian archipelago—presents one of the most enduring paradoxes in cognitive archaeology. While the spectacular figurative depictions of bison, horses, and aurochs have captivated the modern imagination, it is the enigmatic lexicon of abstract signs that offers the most profound window into the prehistoric mind. Paleoanthropologist Genevieve von Petzinger has rigorously documented a core vocabulary of **32 distinct geometric signs** (including aviforms, tectiforms, spirals, and claviforms) that repeat with statistical significance across hundreds of sites.1 Standard archaeological models, including structuralism, shamanic entoptic theories, and proto-writing hypotheses, fail to adequately explain the temporal stability of this system without the presence of institutional mechanisms such as schools, written texts, or centralized priestly castes.

This report investigates the **Correspondential Hypothesis**: the proposition that these 32 signs are not arbitrary cultural conventions, decorative motifs, or neurological accidents, but direct, graphic manifestations of perceived spiritual-natural correspondences. Grounded in the theological epistemology of Emanuel Swedenborg and the cognitive archaeology of the "Memory Code" and "Lithic Scripture," this research posits that the Upper Paleolithic artists were mapping an ontological structure they perceived directly—a mode of cognition Swedenborg terms the "Celestial" state of the "Most Ancient Church".3

The analysis suggests that the emergence of these signs marks a specific "hinge point" in human consciousness—the transition from internal, unmediated perception to externalized, symbolic codification. As direct access to spiritual causality faded—a process theologically termed the "Fall" but cognitively understood as the closing of the internal degree of the mind—humanity developed a "Lithic Scripture" to fix essential cosmological truths in stone and pigment. Detailed correspondential mapping of the 32 signs reveals a coherent theological grammar: **spirals** depict the generative unfolding of divine influx; **aviforms** represent the intellectual principle and the flight of thought 4; and **tectiforms** symbolize the ordering of the human mind and the preservation of the will.

This report provides a systematic catalog of the 32 signs, interpreting them through the Science of Correspondences. It demonstrates that the distribution and stability of these signs are best explained by the stability of the spiritual realities they map, rather than by cultural diffusion alone. Furthermore, it reframes sites like Lascaux, Chauvet, and eventually Göbekli Tepe not as galleries of hunting magic, but as "Crisis Management Diagrams" designed to preserve the "Ancient Word" against the encroaching darkness of the cognitive "Iron Age".3 The findings suggest that the 32 signs constitute a universal symbolic inheritance, likely originating in Africa, which served as the "Operating System" of the prehistoric mind, bridging the gap between the physical landscape and the spiritual causes that sustain it.

**I. The Epistemology of the First Signs**

### **1.1 The Consistency Paradox in Cognitive Archaeology**

The primary anomaly in Paleolithic studies is the "Consistency Paradox." Genevieve von Petzinger’s comprehensive database of geometric signs from 367 European sites (dating c. 40,000–10,000 BCE) reveals that 65% of the sign types remained in use throughout the entire Upper Paleolithic period.2 Even more startling is the finding that two-thirds of these signs were already present when *Homo sapiens* first arrived in Europe, implying a symbolic inheritance that originated in Africa and persisted through the glacial maximum.1 The geographical spread is equally immense, with identical motifs such as hand stencils and scalariforms appearing in sites as distant as Sulawesi, Indonesia, dating to 40,000 years ago.5

Conventional anthropological models struggle to account for this stasis. Cultural conventions are notoriously unstable; linguistic symbols drift, mutate, and disappear over centuries, let alone millennia. The English word "nice" meant "foolish" merely 600 years ago; yet the "tectiform" sign remained recognizable and consistent for over 15,000 years. If these signs were merely decorative (art for art’s sake) or arbitrary markers of clan identity (proto-heraldry), their uniformity across 30,000 years and vast geographical distances is statistically improbable. Standard explanations of cultural transmission require mechanisms of fidelity—schools, books, liturgies—that the Paleolithic record does not evidence in any material form.

The "Entoptic Theory," championed by David Lewis-Williams, attempts to solve this by anchoring the signs in universal neurology—claiming they are phosphenes or form constants seen during trance states induced by sensory deprivation or psychoactive substances.7 However, while this explains the *origin* of the basic shapes (why humans *can* see grids or spirals), it fails to explain their *selection*, *context*, and *meaning*. Why were these specific 32 shapes codified and repeated, while countless other entoptic patterns were ignored? Why are they arranged in specific syntactical relationships with animal figures? The entoptic theory reduces meaning to mechanism, suggesting the signs are essentially hallucinations without semantic content.

The Correspondential Hypothesis resolves this paradox by shifting the locus of stability from culture to ontology. If the signs depict **real correspondences**—immutable causal relationships between spiritual causes and natural effects—then their consistency is inevitable. A spiral corresponds to "generative unfolding" not because a council of elders voted on it, but because that is the actual geometric form of influx in the spiritual world.8 Any human capable of "Celestial Perception" would independently perceive and depict the same form to express the same reality. The consistency is external to the human mind; it belongs to the structure of the cosmos itself.

### **1.2 The Cognitive Shift: From Perception to Codification**

To understand the function of the 32 signs, we must define the cognitive environment of their creators. Drawing on Swedenborg’s anthropology of the "Most Ancient Church" and "Ancient Church," we can distinguish two modes of cognition that overlap during the Upper Paleolithic transition:

#### **The Celestial Mode (Direct Perception)**

This is the baseline state of "Original Participation" (Barfield) or the "Most Ancient Church" (Swedenborg). In this state, the human mind operates "from the whole to the parts." The connection between the natural object and its spiritual cause is immediate and unmediated. There is no need for external symbols because the world itself is the text. A person sees a **mountain** and instantly perceives "Divine Love in an elevated state".3 They see a **bird** and perceive "rational thought" or "intellectual truth".4 This perception is pre-discursive; it does not require reasoning or logic. It is as immediate as seeing color. In this state, "art" as a representational technology is largely unnecessary, as the internal vision is continuous.

#### **The Spiritual Mode (Learned Knowledge)**

As the "Fall" progressed—understood here not as a moral transgression but as a closure of the internal spiritual degree—direct perception faded. Humanity could no longer "see" the spiritual meaning in the object. The link between the mountain and Divine Love was severed in the perception. To preserve the truth, they had to "fix" the correspondence into a static form—a sign.3 This marks the transition to the "Ancient Church" or the Silver Age. The sign becomes a **mnemonic peg**, a container for a truth that is no longer self-evident.

The 32 signs, therefore, are not the *triumph* of abstract thought, but the **compensation** for the loss of direct perception. They are "mnemonics of the spirit," externalized to hold the space where intuition once ruled. The explosion of cave art around 40,000 BCE represents the moment when the "internal internet" flickered out, and humanity began to build an "external hard drive"—the Lithic Scripture. The consistency of the signs is the consistency of a desperate preservation effort, maintaining the "Science of Correspondences" as the only lifeline to a fading spiritual reality.

### **1.3 The "Ancient Word" and the Memory Code**

Functionally, the 32 signs operated as the syntax of the "Ancient Word"—a lost system of symbolic knowledge mentioned by Swedenborg and paralleled in the "Memory Code" research of Lynne Kelly.9 Kelly demonstrates that pre-literate societies encode vast encyclopedias of knowledge into the landscape (songlines) and ritual objects (lukasa boards) to prevent information drift.

In this framework, the geometric signs found in deep cave chambers served as **mnemonic pegs**. A specific **pectiform** (comb-shape) or **scalariform** (ladder-shape) painted in a deep acoustic chamber would act as an index for a specific "chapter" of oral tradition—encoding sequences of spiritual history, astronomical cycles, or the laws of "The Wars of Jehovah" (spiritual combats). The abstract nature of the signs allowed them to be polyvalent, carrying layers of meaning from the pragmatic (animal migration) to the celestial (regeneration of the soul), unified by the law of correspondence. A **zigzag** could simultaneously encode the migration path of herds (natural), the descent of lightning/rain (atmospheric), and the influx of Divine Truth into the mind (spiritual). This multi-level encoding is the hallmark of the "Ancient Word," which Swedenborg claims was written entirely in correspondences.

**II. The 32 Signs: A Correspondential Catalog**

The following catalog synthesizes archaeological description with Swedenborgian correspondential analysis. The mappings are derived from the principle that **function dictates correspondence**. The shape and behavior of the natural analogue reveal the spiritual reality it embodies. The list organizes the signs into functional categories based on their correspondential utility.

### **Category A: The Geometry of Influx and Process**

*Signs representing movement, creation, the flow of life, and the dynamics of the spirit.*

#### **1\. The Spiral**

* **Form Description:** A coiled line, appearing in both clockwise and counter-clockwise directions. Can be simple or interconnected (meanders).  
* **Distribution:** Found in Upper Paleolithic sites but explodes in frequency during the Neolithic (e.g., Newgrange). Present in French Magdalenian sites.10  
* **Natural Analogue:** Vine tendrils, snail shells, whirlpools, galaxies, hurricanes.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Generative Process & Divine Influx.** The spiral is the geometry of the "gyre"—the form of flux in the spiritual world. Swedenborg describes the "flux" of heaven as spiral gyres, and the process of regeneration as a spiraling ascent. It represents the unfolding of creation from the center (Divine) to the circumference (Nature) and the return ascent.  
* **Context Analysis:** The appearance of spirals in "deep" contexts suggests their role in marking the "entry point" of spiritual travel or the beginning of a creative cycle. It maps the movement of the soul through discrete degrees.

#### **2\. The Zigzag (Chevron)**

* **Form Description:** A continuous angular line, often repeated in parallel series.  
* **Distribution:** Extremely common across all periods and regions. Etched on shells by *Homo erectus* 500,000 years ago.11  
* **Natural Analogue:** Lightning, river meanders, snakes, mountain peaks.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Divine Truth Descending / Revelation.** The zigzag mimics the flash of lightning, which corresponds to the sudden illumination of truth piercing the obscurity of the natural mind.12 It represents the descent of the Divine through discrete degrees—a "stepping down" of voltage to be received by the earth.  
* **Context Analysis:** Often found near "wounded men" or scenes of crisis, representing the "lightning flash" of judgment or the influx of truth that initiates spiritual combat.

#### **3\. The Serpentiform**

* **Form Description:** An undulating, wavy line, distinct from the sharp angles of the zigzag.  
* **Distribution:** Widespread, often found near the ground level of cave walls.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Snake, stream, intestines.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **The Sensual Mind.** The "serpent" corresponds to the sensual/corporeal degree of the mind—the part that is in contact with the earth. In order, it is wisdom ("be ye wise as serpents"); in disorder, it is "persuasive falsity" or the deception of the senses.4  
* **Context Analysis:** Its placement often delimits the "lower" faculties. As a stream, it corresponds to the flow of knowledge; as a snake, to the boundary of the ego/proprium.

#### **4\. The Line**

* **Form Description:** A single straight stroke, often grouped in parallel series or marks.  
* **Distribution:** The most frequent sign, present at \>70% of sites.10  
* **Natural Analogue:** Horizon, path, reed, boundary.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Terminus / Boundary / Singular Truth.** A line defines a limit. In the spiritual world, lines mark the boundaries between societies or states. Parallel lines (finger fluting) represent the "flow" of perception or the ordering of truths.10  
* **Context Analysis:** Used to delimit space, count states (days/moons), or mark the boundaries of a sacred precinct.

#### **5\. Finger Fluting (Macaroni)**

* **Form Description:** Parallel lines traced by fingers in soft clay or moonmilk, often meandering or interlacing.  
* **Distribution:** Found in deep, hard-to-access chambers (e.g., Rouffignac).  
* **Natural Analogue:** Water flow, furrows, hair.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Flow of Perception.** The immediate, tactile reception of truth; "living water." Unlike the rigid line, fluting is organic and fluid, representing the "stream of providence" or the continuous influx of life.14  
* **Context Analysis:** The tactile nature suggests an attempt to physically touch the "substance" of the earth-mother, verifying the reality of the spiritual ground.

### **Category B: The Architecture of the Mind (The Will)**

*Signs representing structures, containers, and states of the will/heart.*

#### **6\. The Tectiform**

* **Form Description:** A roof-like shape, often with a central pillar and base. "Pentiform" or hut-shape.  
* **Distribution:** Highly localized in the Dordogne/Lot region (Magdalenian). A marker of specific cultural/spiritual identity.10  
* **Natural Analogue:** Hut, tent, house, trap.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **The Human Will / The Mind.** In correspondences, a "house" is the human mind, with its rooms representing different affections. The "roof" is the ruling love that protects the interiors. A tectiform represents the "house of the soul"—a state of good that contains and protects truth.  
* **Context Analysis:** Its late appearance (Magdalenian) suggests a shift from "roaming" (nomadic perception) to "dwelling" (fixed doctrine). It represents the construction of a doctrinal system to house the spiritual life.

#### **7\. The Spanish Tectiform**

* **Form Description:** A larger, rectangular, compartmentalized grid, distinct from the French roof-shape.  
* **Distribution:** Northern Spain (e.g., El Castillo, La Pasiega).  
* **Natural Analogue:** Complex dwelling, field partitions, fortress.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Doctrine / Systematic Order.** The compartmentalization suggests the ordering of the mind into distinct chambers or degrees—the "many mansions" of the internal mind. It represents a more complex, rigid doctrinal structure than the French variant.

#### **8\. The Quadrangle**

* **Form Description:** Square, rectangle, or trapezoid.  
* **Distribution:** Widespread.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Four-sided enclosure, field.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Justice / Celestial State.** Swedenborg explicitly links the "square" (as in the New Jerusalem) to **Justice** and the union of Good and Truth. Four sides facing the four quarters (North, South, East, West) signifies impartiality, completeness, and a state of being "square" with Divine Order.16  
* **Context Analysis:** Represents a state of settled order and moral rectitude, contrasting with the fluid organic shapes of the spiral.

#### **9\. The Cupule**

* **Form Description:** Small circular depression pounded into rock.  
* **Distribution:** One of the oldest signs, found globally (Lower Paleolithic).  
* **Natural Analogue:** Cup, mortar, womb, navel.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Receptivity.** The state of being open to receive influx. The "vessel" of the heart or will that receives Divine Love. It is the fundamental "will to receive".18  
* **Context Analysis:** The act of pounding cupules is rhythmic and auditory, suggesting a ritual of opening the earth (the natural mind) to receive sound/spirit.

#### **10\. The Reniform**

* **Form Description:** Kidney-shaped.  
* **Distribution:** Less common; found in French caves.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Kidney, bean.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Purification / Separation / Truth Searching.** The kidneys function to separate the pure blood from the impure serum. Reniform signs correspond to the faculty of **Judgment**—distinguishing pure truth from falsity and removing the "urinous" (falsified) elements.20  
* **Context Analysis:** Likely marks zones of "testing" or purification in the initiatory journey.

#### **11\. The Cordiform**

* **Form Description:** Heart-shaped.  
* **Distribution:** Rare.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Heart.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Celestial Love / The Will.** The heart corresponds to the Will and the vital heat of love. A cordiform sign marks the presence of the "Celestial" principle—love to the Lord.21

### **Category C: The Intellectual Principle (The Birds)**

*Signs representing thought, reason, the understanding, and the elevation of mind.*

#### **12\. The Aviform**

* **Form Description:** "Placard" shape; a horizontal bracket with a central stem, resembling a bird in flight.  
* **Distribution:** Found in Pech Merle, Cougnac, Cosquer.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Bird in flight, wings.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Intellectual Ideas / Thoughts.** Birds correspond to things of the understanding—rational concepts that can "fly" above the earth (the will). The Aviform represents the **Intellectual Principle**.4  
* **Context Analysis:** Frequently associated with "Wounded Men" (e.g., at Pech Merle). This confirms the Swedenborgian interpretation of these scenes as **Spiritual Temptations**—battles of the intellect against falsity, rather than literal murders. The intellect (bird) is the weapon and the victim in this internal war.

#### **13\. The Penniform**

* **Form Description:** Feather-like, branching lines (barbed sign).  
* **Distribution:** Common (25% of sites). Found on the "Chinese Horse" at Lascaux.23  
* **Natural Analogue:** Feather, branch, arrow fletching, tree.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Sensual Truths / Scientifics.** Feathers "clothe" the bird just as sensual truths (scientific facts) clothe the spiritual understanding. They also aid flight. Penniforms represent the "external truths" that protect and adorn the mind.  
* **Context Analysis:** When on an animal, it signifies the "clothing" of that affection with truth. When freestanding, it may represent the "Tree of Knowledge" or the proliferation of scientifics.

#### **14\. The Flabelliform**

* **Form Description:** Fan-shaped; radiating lines from a base.  
* **Distribution:** Less common.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Hand fan, radiating light, spreading feathers.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Opening of the Mind / Respiration.** The fan moves air (spirit). It corresponds to the **expansion** of the understanding and the reception of new truths. It is the "breath" of the spirit.24

#### **15\. The Y-Sign**

* **Form Description:** Forked line; Y-shape.  
* **Distribution:** Widespread.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Tree crotch, path divergence.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Choice / Bifurcation.** The point of decision between two paths (Good vs. Evil, Truth vs. Falsity). Also corresponds to the "opening" of a degree.25  
* **Context Analysis:** Recent research links Y-signs to biological cycles (birthing), suggesting a link to "bringing forth" or production.26 In correspondence, this is the "bringing forth" of good in act.

### **Category D: The Agents of Power and Presence**

*Signs representing agency, distinctness, power, and the Divine Human.*

#### **16\. The Claviform**

* **Form Description:** Club-shaped; a vertical line with a lateral bulge/bump. P-shaped.  
* **Distribution:** Common in Magdalenian; present in 16% of French sites.27  
* **Natural Analogue:** Club, weapon, stylized female figure.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Power of Truth / The Will.** A club is a weapon of the hand (power). It corresponds to the **power of the literal sense** of the Word—truth used to strike down falsity. Also linked to the "rod of iron" (ruling truth).  
* **Context Analysis:** Often paired with horses or bison. Represents the power to command or direct the natural affections.

#### **17\. The Hand Stencil (Negative Hand)**

* **Form Description:** Outline of a hand created by blowing pigment.  
* **Distribution:** Universal and ancient.  
* **Natural Analogue:** The human hand.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Power / Presence / The Proprium.** The hand corresponds to **power** and ability. The *negative* stencil (absence of the hand surrounded by pigment) is profound: it suggests the **acknowledgment of the self's nothingness** before the Divine. The "proprium" (selfhood) is removed, leaving only the trace of presence.6  
* **Context Analysis:** "I was here, but I am nothing." A mark of witness and humility, or an invocation of power.

#### **18\. The Positive Hand (Handprint)**

* **Form Description:** Paint applied to hand and pressed on wall.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Appropriation / Will.** Touching signifies communication and transfer. A positive print suggests **appropriating** the state of the cave/animal—making it one's own. It is an act of will and possession.6

#### **19\. The Cruciform**

* **Form Description:** Cross (+ or x).  
* **Distribution:** Rare (13% of sites).  
* **Natural Analogue:** Intersection, star.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Conjunction / Temptation.** The marriage of Good (vertical) and Truth (horizontal). Also represents the state of **temptation** (the cross to be borne) which leads to conjunction.29

#### **20\. The Dot / Disk**

* **Form Description:** Solid point or circle.  
* **Distribution:** Oldest sign (El Castillo, 41k BP).  
* **Natural Analogue:** Seed, sun, eye, pupil.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **The Beginning / The One / Divine Good.** The dot is the center from which all geometry unfolds. It corresponds to the **First Point** of creation and the "purity" of good. A red disk corresponds to the **Sun of Heaven** (Divine Love).28

#### **21\. The Triangle**

* **Form Description:** Three-sided polygon.  
* **Natural Analogue:** Mountain, arrowhead, pubic triangle.  
* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** **Scientific Truth / Elevation.** The number 3 corresponds to **fullness**, specifically regarding truth. An upward triangle is elevation to the Divine (mountain); a downward triangle is influx or the feminine principle (womb/receptivity). The triangle maps the "trinity" of End, Cause, and Effect.28

#### **27\. The W-Sign**

#### **30\. The Half-Circle**

**III. Pattern Analysis: Syntax of the Stone Library**

The Correspondential Hypothesis is validated not just by individual sign meanings, but by their *syntactic* combination. Paleolithic art is not a random collection of doodles; it is a "Lithic Scripture" with a coherent narrative structure.

### **3.1 The Syntax of Crisis: The Aviform and the Spiral**

One of the most potent combinations identified in the research is the pairing of **Aviforms** with **Spirals** or meanders.4

* **Correspondential Reading:** The **Spiral** represents the entrance to the spiritual world (the gyre of vastation or regeneration). The **Aviform** (Bird) represents the intellectual principle—the part of the human mind capable of detaching from the body and "flying" into these upper realms.  
* **Narrative:** This combination diagrams the "Ecstatic Journey" or "Regeneration." It depicts the intellect entering the spiral of spiritual evolution. In the Lascaux Shaft Scene, the bird-headed man falling (or flying) next to the wounded bison represents the **separation of the Intellect from the Will**—a specific theological necessity described by Swedenborg for the salvation of the "Ancient Church".4 The Will (Bison) is mortally wounded (corrupt), but the Intellect (Bird) survives to receive a new Conscience.

### **3.2 The Syntax of Order: Tectiforms and Dots**

Tectiforms (houses) are frequently associated with rows of dots or lines.

* **Correspondential Reading:** If the Tectiform is the "House of the Mind," the dots represent **states of life** or specific truths (knowledges) stored within it. The organization of dots into rows (lines) signifies the ordering of these knowledges into a coherent doctrine.15  
* **Implication:** This is not just a picture of a hut; it is a diagram of the "organized mind." The shift from loose dots (Aurignacian) to structured Tectiforms (Magdalenian) traces the evolution from "perception" (fluid) to "doctrine" (structured).9

### **3.3 Spatial Distribution Patterns**

The research highlights a critical distinction between "Entrance" and "Deep Chamber" signs.3

* **Entrance/Daylight Zone:** Dominated by "public" iconography—large animals, open angles, lines. These correspond to **Natural Truths**—the external, behavioral laws of the community (the "literal sense" of the Word).  
* **Deep/Dark Zone:** Dominated by complex signs (Tectiforms, Aviforms, Spirals) and composite figures (The Sorcerer). These correspond to **Spiritual/Celestial Truths**—the interior arcana of regeneration and the soul’s interaction with the Divine.  
* **The Descent:** The physical journey into the cave corresponds to the introspection of the mind. The "Sanctuary" at Trois-Frères, located at the end of a difficult spiraling tunnel, physically enforces the "gyre" required to access the "inner man".3

### **3.4 Temporal Patterns: The Fall and Codification**

The research suggests a temporal trajectory.

* **Aurignacian (40k-28k BP):** Predominance of Dots, Hands, and simple signs. Focus on **Presence** and **Origin** (The Dot). Direct perception is still active; signs are simple anchors.  
* **Gravettian (28k-22k BP):** Explosion of Hand Stencils and more complex signs. The "Crisis" of the Fall begins; the need to mark the "self" (Hand) becomes urgent.  
* **Magdalenian (17k-10k BP):** Peak complexity. Tectiforms, Claviforms, Aviforms. Knowledge is now fully **codified** into a rigid system. Perception is lost; Doctrine (Tectiform) rules. This aligns with the "Iron Age" of the cycle, where truth is hard, fixed, and weaponized (Claviform).10
