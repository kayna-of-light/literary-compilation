# Theme 002 — The Swedenborgian layer as a filter

## The author's reading (2026-10-24)

"They recognized the structure in his teachings that was preserved for a long time through paths like the hermetics, so that work was already done … they only needed to 'fix' it towards Swedenborg." Clarified: "I didn't mean they edited him. I simply meant they added a layer on top of it that acted as a sort of filter."

Like the author's own reading of the Nag Hammadi texts, except that those texts were written *in* correspondence and Delsarte's system only *approached* it.

## Does the record support it?

**The structure came down the hermetic path.**
- Delsarte's sources are the Emerald Tablet (in MacKaye's notebook), Kabbalah through illuminist freemasonry, and Aquinas (Waille 2012, 2015; stores 003, 004).
- Stebbins says the same: his philosophy is "a compound of cabalistic lore and Platonism"; his system is "based on the so-called hermetic doctrine of correspondences" (store 006).

**The expositors added a layer and did not edit the system.**
- Stebbins keeps Delsarte's own fragments, the Philotechnic address and his charts.
- Over them she lays:
  - Swedenborg's three degrees of conatus, force and motion (*DLW* §219), as the order of will, body and action;
  - Madeley's New Church definition of what correspondence is;
  - Swedenborg's "love, wisdom and power" as the name of the trinity.
- Monroe's "Swedenborg geometrized" is the same layer in four words.
- Northrop states it as the reading for the general public: "the law of correspondence, which was discovered by Swedenborg".

**The layer worked as a filter downstream.**
- *The renaming.* The hermetic "correspondences" (plural) became the "Law of Correspondence" (singular) in the American line (Waille 2012, n. 30). In English, "correspondence" in the singular, as a law, was the form in which Swedenborg's doctrine was read in nineteenth-century America.
- *Volkonsky.* He learned Delsarte from this textbook. He saw the layer ("from Pythagoras to Swedenborg") and set the philosophy aside, keeping "the manifestation of the inner man in his outward form".
- *Stanislavsky and the studio.* What came down from there was the content without the names.

## The feature test (stores 017, 018)

Does Delsarte's own system hold a feature found only in Swedenborg? No. Its Thomist markers ("the soul being the form of the body"; nature and grace) and its body map (mind in the head, love in the torso, as in the Zohar) are not Swedenborg's. In the framework's terms this is a Tier 3 system: a detailed key with a different organization (*Transmission of the Ancient Word* §VII.D).

What the expositors supplied is precisely the Swedenborgian part: the order of conatus, force and motion; correspondence as "no arbitrary relationship"; and the law in the singular.

## What passed to the studio

What the studio received matches the layer, not Delsarte's Thomist frame:
- the one-way order of inner cause and outer effect ("the mind is the pilot"; "emotion and mood … first"), as theme_003 shows;
- the triad of inner forces (*An Actor Prepares*);
- function as the ground of form (the art books).

None of Delsarte's Catholic theology and none of his Kabbalistic body map reached the 1937 class. Stanchfield's mime lecture later carried the three centres in a popular form.

## Status

The filter reading is **interpretation**, and it is supported by the record at every point where the record speaks. It is not a claim that the expositors altered Delsarte's text, and it is not a claim that the studio knew the layer was Swedenborgian.
