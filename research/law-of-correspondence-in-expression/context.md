# Context: what the library already holds

Every claim below traces to a document in `data/`, with its section. This is the library as it stood when this research began (2026-10-24, after PR #51).

## 1. The framework the research works inside

**Correspondence is ontological, and access to it varies.** *The Transmission of the Ancient Word* (`data/00_Framework/`) §II.A–B argues that correspondence is law, whether or not anyone reads it. What varies across history is access to the key. It names three routes of access (§II.E):

- the lost intuitive faculty;
- transmission of the codified system;
- direct spiritual instruction.

**The quality gradient** (§§VII, X):

- **Tier 3, fragmentary access.** Hermeticism keeps "the principle itself" but "not the dictionary" (§VII.B). Kabbalah is "parallel development with different organization": a detailed key, but organized on the Tree of Life and overlapping the Swedenborgian system "significantly but not completely" (§VII.D). The quality marker of Tier 3 is "inability to construct" (§VII.G).
- **Restoration.** Swedenborg restores the key (§VIII.D): "the systematic, transmittable key that restores the ability to read correspondence at the spiritual and natural degrees."
- **Tier 4, re-emergence in culture.** Grimm, Andersen, Carroll and Disney (§IX). For Disney the vectors named are source material and personnel (§IX.D). Pedagogy is not among them.

**The hermetic line.** *The Golden Chain of Prisca Theologia* (`data/06_Mythological_Studies/`) Part IV.4.1 calls Swedenborg's doctrine of correspondences "a direct elaboration of the Hermetic 'As above, so below'". It also distinguishes Hermetic *sympathy* (manipulation) from Swedenborgian *signification* (reading).

**Kabbalah as a parallel witness.** *The Hermeneutics of the Mundus Imaginalis* (`data/02_Swedenborgian_Theology/`) §5.2 tabulates Adam Kadmon / Maximus Homo and two further parallels. It concludes that the convergence of Philo, the Zohar and Swedenborg shows "a perennial hermeneutic tradition". §2.2 gives the criterion used throughout the Disney work: a correspondence is "validated by the functional analogy of the object itself", an allegory by "a key or codebook".

**Blavatsky's equation.** *A Cultural History* §3.2 quotes Blavatsky (1877, 1:306): Swedenborg's "doctrine of correspondence, or Hermetic symbolism, is that of Pythagoras and of the kabalists—'as above, so below'". The same equation of Swedenborg with the hermetic and Pythagorean line appears in Stebbins and in Volkonsky (store 006, 009). It was a commonplace of the period.

## 2. The studio's method, as the library records it

**The memo.** Walt Disney to Don Graham, 23 December 1935: "the driving force behind the action is the mood, the personality, the attitude of the character—or all three. Therefore, the mind is the pilot. We think of things before the body does them." It also says "there is a scientific approach to this business". Sources: *The Mechanics of the Soul* §§2.2–2.3; *The Crisis of Ensoulment* §3.1, which has the fuller opening, "what the force behind the movement is".

**Graham's classes.** Graham, 21 June 1937: "The artist is not drawing a form at all, but indicating a force"; "The vital element is force" (*Mechanics* §3.1; *Crisis* §3.2). Graham, 22 February 1937: "all primary actions are the result of thought processes" (*Mechanics* §3.2). *The Diffracted Light* §2.3 adds the further lines read on the scans:

- "It *is* a philosophy";
- "not really new, but adapted to Studio uses";
- Tytla's "We can't be mystical … This is a business".

**Stanislavsky as a parallel.** *Mechanics* §V treats Stanislavsky as a parallel, with Tytla's reading of Boleslavsky (Crafton 2013) and "Inner Motive Forces" set beside the memo. It presents this as "shared DNA" and does not trace a route.

**Continuity.** Stanchfield, *Drawn to Life* ("The Inner Force", "The Metaphysical Side"): *Mechanics* §§4.2, VI; *Invisible Influx* §5.2. The 1995 reissue of the memo and NYU's Action Analysis: *Invisible Influx* §5.3.

**The Revival art books.** *Diffracted Light* §§IV–VII (rewritten 2026-10-24) shows the method in the art books of *Moana*, *Frozen II* and *Encanto*. The artists reason from function, force and inner state, and none rests a symbol on cultural authority.

## 3. The route, as the library explained it before this research

**Atmospheric route.** Chouinard was "permeable to Theosophical and Metaphysical ideas" (Karlstrom 1996; Crosse 2014). Kandinsky: "form is the outward expression of this inner meaning". Sources: *Mechanics* §4.2; *Invisible Influx* §5.2; *Diffracted Light* §2.3, second paragraph; *Parsimony* §3.5. No document shows Graham reading Theosophy.

**The gap stated.** *A Cultural History* §4.4: "we possess no document where Don Graham writes 'I am teaching Swedenborg'". It prefers "organic selection": Graham "formalized intuitions that happened to align with Swedenborgian principles".

**The cumulative case.** *The Parsimony Problem* §3.2 (pedagogical strand) calls the isomorphism "documented fact" but leaves the route as "conscious transmission or parallel invention". Its Future Directions (§4.7, item 3) ask for Graham's papers.

**The one paragraph that held the chain.** *Diffracted Light* §2.3, added 2026-10-24:

- Delsarte's Law of Correspondence;
- Waille's judgement that Delsarte was not Swedenborg's disciple;
- the American expositors naming Swedenborg (Stebbins, with *DLW* §219 and Madeley; Monroe's "Swedenborg geometrized"; Northrop);
- Hovey in Los Angeles;
- Volkonsky's use of Stebbins;
- Stanislavsky through Volkonsky (Whyman 2008);
- *An Actor Prepares* recommended in the 1937 class;
- Stanchfield's Delsarte lecture.

It does not carry the feature test, the author's filter reading, the Russian evidence of Autant-Mathieu, or the "inner motive forces".

## 4. What the library gets wrong or leaves loose on this subject

- *Mechanics* §4.2 and *Diffracted Light* §2.3 (second paragraph) lean on the atmospheric Theosophy route, which has no document behind it for Graham. The documented route is the expression tradition.
- *Mechanics* §V calls Stanislavsky's "Inner Motive Forces" "functionally identical" to the "Ruling Love". In *An Actor Prepares* the inner motive forces are three: mind, will and feeling (store 012). The comparison that holds is with the Delsartean trinity, and through Stebbins with Swedenborg's will and understanding issuing in action (*DLW* §219).
- No library document records that the Leonardo/memo joining quoted in *Drawn to Life* comes from Eric Larson's notes (store 015).
