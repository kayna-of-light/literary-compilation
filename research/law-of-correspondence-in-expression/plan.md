# The Law of Correspondence in the Arts of Expression: research plan

**Started**: 2026-10-24, at the author's request after the nightly audit of 2026-10-24 (PR #51). In that audit the chain was found and set down as one dense paragraph in *The Diffracted Light* §2.3. The rest was kept in `docs/disney_cumulative_case_map.md`, which is a working file.

## The question

The library's Disney case holds that the studio's method ("the mind is the pilot"; "the artist is not drawing a form at all, but indicating a force") is the doctrine of influx and correspondence in the form of a craft. Until now, the library has explained how the method reached the studio in two ways:

- **Atmospheric**: the Theosophy and New Thought of Los Angeles, Chouinard, Kandinsky.
- **Structural**: the method and the doctrine have the same shape.

*A Cultural History* §4.4 names the result: "the evidentiary gap: no documented Swedenborg connection."

The author put a sharper question (2026-10-24). The studio's artists do not describe "emotion put into action", which is a commonplace. They describe "inner spiritual function expressed in outer form", reached from what the thing does. Where does *that* come from? Is there a missing link in the chain?

The author's claim, stated exactly, is what this research tests:

1. The method's core is the science of correspondences that Swedenborg rediscovered.
2. Swedenborgianism was present at the studio's beginning and most likely gave the studio its shape.
3. The impulse of the method came from Swedenborg.
4. It is taught as craft, not as a key of meanings.

The claim is **not** that the artists knew Swedenborg.

Two parts must be kept apart:

1. **The record.** Link by link, what passes from Swedenborg to the studio's classes, in what words, by whose hand, and at what strength?
2. **The reading.** What the record means in the framework's terms: the hermetic and Kabbalistic preservation of the principle (Tier 3 of the quality gradient in *The Transmission of the Ancient Word*), the restored key (Swedenborg), and the reading of the one through the other.

## Not reached (stated, not guessed)

- Waille's thesis (Lyon 3, 2009; ANRT 2011) and Porte's anthology (*François Delsarte: une anthologie*, 1992). These would give the French original of Stebbins's "Love, Wisdom, Power" fragment.
- The LSU Delsarte Collection finding aid.
- *Theatre Workshop* 1936–37, the quarterly the 1937 class recommended. Not online.
- Ruyter, *Reformers and Visionaries* (1979) and *The Cultivation of Body and Mind in Nineteenth-Century American Delsartism* (1999). Not read; quoted only through Waille.
- Fischer (2013) on Peirce and Delsarte. The KU repository refused the download.
- Walt Disney Archives transcripts not published by Hans Perk (Graham, 7 June 1937; Pearce, "Acting and Pantomime", 9 March 1939).
- Graham, *Composing Pictures* (1970). Not online.
- Taylor and Whyman, "François Delsarte, Prince Sergei Volkonski and Mikhail Chekhov", *Mime Journal* (2004–5). Cited by Autant-Mathieu; not read.
