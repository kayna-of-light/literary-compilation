# Source 011 — Swedenborg: the loci used in the Carroll rewrite

**Editions** (Swedenborg Foundation Standard Edition PDFs, extracted with pypdfium2; *HH* cross-checked on e-swedenborg, Ager): *Heaven and Hell*, trans. John C. Ager (2009); *Arcana Coelestia*, trans. John Clowes, rev. John Faulkner Potts, 12 vols (2009); *Apocalypse Revealed*, trans. John Whitehead, 2 vols (2009); *Divine Love and Wisdom*, trans. John C. Ager (2009).
**Accessed**: 2026-10-21/23. Every section number below was located by its section heading in the text.
