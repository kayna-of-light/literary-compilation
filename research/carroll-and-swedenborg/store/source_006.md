# Source 006 — Kegler, "Symbolist Landscapes: with reference to Sheridan Le Fanu and George MacDonald" (2005)

**Citation**: Kegler, Adelheid. "Symbolist Landscapes: With Reference to Sheridan Le Fanu and George MacDonald." Translated by Alan Stott. In *Between Method and Madness: Essays on Swedenborg and Literature*, edited by Stephen McNeilly, 45–. Journal of the Swedenborg Society 4. London: Swedenborg Society, 2005. **Accessed**: 2026-10-23, archive.org FTS `betweenmethodmad0000unse`.

## Findings
- The volume's introduction: Kegler studies "the phantasy writing of George MacDonald. The influence of Swedenborg is of special interest here, she writes, because he offers a model of the …" (sentence continues; not fully read).
- Kegler reads *Phantastes* and *Lilith* in Swedenborgian terms: "In *Phantastes*, the Divine Human (Blake's Swedenborgian vocabulary) is shown to Anodos's inner …"; the Symbolist landscape "represents the 'Grand Man' as the anticipation of eternity".
- Her notes cite her own German study: "Die Verschmelzung traditionaler und Swedenborgianischer Denkstrukturen in M.s *Lilith*", *Inklings Jahrbuch* 2002 (not read).
- The contributors' note on MacDonald: "His friends included Lewis Carroll, John Ruskin and Frederick Denison Maurice."

## Why it matters
Independent scholarship (published by the Swedenborg Society) on Swedenborgian thought-structures in MacDonald's fantasy. It strengthens the MacDonald node; it says nothing about Carroll's own reading.
