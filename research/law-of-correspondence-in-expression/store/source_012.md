# Source 012 — Stanislavski, *An Actor Prepares* (1936): the inner motive forces

**Citation**: Stanislavski, Constantin. *An Actor Prepares.* Translated by Elizabeth Reynolds Hapgood. New York: Theatre Arts, 1936.

**Accessed**: 2026-10-24, archive.org full-text search, item `actorprepares00cons` (lending copy; phrase-probed).

## What the text says

**Chapter Twelve is "Inner Motive Forces" (p. 229)**; the contents list shows "xii. Inner Motive Forces 229".
- "… turns to his spiritual and physical creative instrument. His mind, will, and feelings combine to mobilize all of …" (p. 230; the sentence continues beyond what the probe returns).
- "Since these three forces form a triumvirate, inextricably bound up together, what you …"
- "… reciprocal interaction of the members of this triumvirate in order not only to arouse them by natural …"
- "… we can turn to one member of the triumvirate, perhaps the mind, because it responds …"

The book also speaks throughout of "inner and outer" life: "only when an actor feels that his inner and outer life on the stage is flowing naturally …".

## Why it matters

- **This is the book the Disney class recommended** in February 1937 (store 014).
- **Its motive forces are a triad.** Mind, will and feeling, "a triumvirate, inextricably bound up together", moving the "spiritual and physical creative instrument". Leabhart and Merlin set this triad beside the Delsartean trinity (store 010, n. 3), and Stebbins had read that trinity through Swedenborg's will and understanding issuing in action (*DLW* §219; store 006).
- **This corrects the library.** *Mechanics of the Soul* §V compares the "Inner Motive Forces" to the "Ruling Love". The comparison that holds is triad to triad: Stanislavsky's mind, will and feeling; Delsarte's mind, soul and life; Swedenborg's understanding and will issuing in action.

## Caution

A full-text probe for "Delsarte" in *An Actor Prepares* returns nothing: Stanislavsky does not name him there. The link runs through Volkonsky's courses and book (stores 009–011).
