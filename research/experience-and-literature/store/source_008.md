## What they say

**Brown (ethnography: interviews with channels and participant observation at sessions and workshops).**
- Channels "gradually moved from trance channeling to conscious channeling as their understanding matured" (verified).
- Channeling is taught. Brown cites a text titled "Channeling—A Natural Skill Easily Learned" (verified). Classes and workshops are described (agent): a nationally known trance channel "outlined his concepts on a flip chart, turning the workshop into a New Age chalk-talk."
- Sessions are built on audience questions (agent): J. Z. Knight "answers questions posed by members of the audience."
- Source labels are interchangeable (agent): for one channel, "'being,' 'energy,' 'higher self,' 'collective unconscious'" were "in no way incompatible."
- The receiver is exalted (agent): channelled declarations such as "You are totality. You are God, operating on a particular resonance." Critics see "narcissism, political complacency, or victim-blaming"; religion "reduced to a technology of personal empowerment—something to be sold in workshops."
- The book's blurb describes Brown as "neither a debunker nor an advocate."

**Hastings (a sympathetic transpersonal psychologist)** (agent).
- Automatic writing involves "a slight dissociation."
- He weighs an "unconscious personification" model of the source and grants that it fits many cases, but not all.
- He names the "opposite effect of too little dissociation, in which the messages of the entity reflect elements of the person's desires."
- He lists the risks of channeling as "dependence, inflation, and lack of balance."
- A section headed "PROPHECIES THAT FAILED" includes Cayce's "in the year 1970 there shall be earth changes."

**Hanegraaff** (agent). He analyses "You are God" teachings under the heading "Psychologization of Religion and Sacralization of Psychology," and qualifies them: Ramtha's "You are God" "is slightly overstated: at most, Ramtha claims that 'You are a God.'"

## Why it matters

- **Production.** Channelling, in the scholarship, is a practice: learned in classes, entered on purpose, run on audience questions, shaped by what the channel brings. A sympathetic researcher (Hastings) names the leak of the channel's own desires into the messages and the risk of "inflation." This is the mode the project calls *produced*.
- **Direction.** Brown and Hanegraaff both record the "you are God" axis in channelled teaching. This gives published support for the lineage claim in *The Empty Room* §14, which the library states without citation.
