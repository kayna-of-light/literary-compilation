# Context: what the library said about Carroll (before 2026-10-23)

Every Carroll passage in `data/` was read in full. Line numbers refer to the files as they stood on `dev` at 6077f83.

## The claims, by document

**`data/07_Cultural_Pneumatology/The Looking-Glass of Theology…`** (whole document). Thesis: Carroll's works are "rigorous, systematic applications of the Doctrine of Correspondences" (§I); conclusion: "Lewis Carroll was a systematic Swedenborgian thinker in the guise of a nonsense poet" (§VIII). Load-bearing claims:
- §2.1: his library held *Heaven and Hell*, *Divine Love and Wisdom*, *Arcana Coelestia* (raw source 1: a University of Reading special-collections PDF). Corrected 2026-10-21/22 to the three Lovett entries, but the paragraph still argued from the library.
- §2.2: MacDonald's children urged publication (raw source 4: a Guideposts web article); MacDonald "knew prominent Swedenborgians such as Garth Wilkinson" (raw source 5: a Dundee PhD, not read).
- §2.3: Maurice's *aionios* is "purely Swedenborgian"; "Karen Gardiner's recent thesis" (raw source 8: a blog post about the thesis).
- §2.4: Hughes, Wilkinson, "spirit drawing" (raw sources 10–11: a local-history page, a paper on Georgiana Houghton). No record links Carroll to Wilkinson's spirit-drawing.
- §§III–VII: correspondences sourced largely to web pages unrelated to Swedenborg (Hudson Tuttle's spiritualist *Arcana of Nature* for the rabbit; a Gnostic-psychology lecture site for the cat; a witchcraft blog for fungi; ResearchGate "Dialectics with Claude").
- §6.1: the *Sylvie and Bruno Concluded* preface is "a direct transliteration of Swedenborg's description of his own states". The preface names its own model: "such as we meet with in 'Esoteric Buddhism'" (source_009).

**`…/The Ontology of the Riddle…`**. A correspondential reading of the Hatter's riddle (raven and desk as the natural mind: "flat" and "without life of their own"). Claims about Carroll: §4.4 argues Carroll "literally did not answer the riddle", that "as originally invented" is "evasive", that the answer is a "performative decoy", and (§4.4.5) an "esoteric concealment hypothesis": Carroll "constructed a precise theological puzzle … a masterful evasion that has fooled scholars for over a century". §4.2 reads Noah's raven as "the avatar of the unregenerate mind", at home in the desolation; Swedenborg reads it as falsities that still disturb a man *being* regenerated (*AC* §§864–867, source_011).

**`…/The Animating Spirit_ A Cultural History…`** §5.3 (rewritten 2026-10-21): the three Lovett entries; "Carroll's interpretation of Swedenborg was mediated through George MacDonald"; Maurice's distinction "purely Swedenborgian". §9.1 item 4: Grimm, Andersen and Carroll "carried documented Swedenborgian transmission through Schelling, Ørsted, and MacDonald/Maurice".

**`data/00_Master_Theses/The River and the Vessel…`**: abstract item 4 and §§ 82, 454, 696, 700, 726, 770 — Carroll among carriers "receiving correspondential content through documented transmission channels"; "Carroll transmitted what he read".

**`data/00_Framework/The Transmission of the Ancient Word…`** §IX.C and the §IX.E table: Carroll's access "Direct (owned texts, MacDonald)", quality "Construction", "complete systematic access"; §XII item 3 lists Carroll among those with "complete access". Works Cited 29 gives the Gardiner thesis as "Theology within the Nonsense: Lewis Carroll and the Eschatology of State", PhD, Bristol, 2018 — the real thesis is Nottingham, 2023, with a different title (source_008).

**`data/00_Master_Theses/The Beast That Wears the Lamb…`** l. 610 already states the position the record supports: "These writers did not necessarily know what they were carrying. But the Lord's truth flows through open vessels regardless of whether the vessel can name what flows through it." No change needed.

## What the library's own framework says about routes

*The Transmission of the Ancient Word* (§ Thesis, l. 16) holds that correspondence is "ontological law (not invented by any human tradition)" and that access to it can come by "(a) the lost intuitive faculty … (b) transmission of the codified system, or (c) direct spiritual instruction". *The River and the Vessel* argues that content grounded in reality "does not drift because each generation's lived experience re-confirms the structure". The library therefore does not need a Swedenborgian channel to read Carroll correspondentially; it needs the channel only to claim *transmission*. That distinction is the hinge of the rewrite.
