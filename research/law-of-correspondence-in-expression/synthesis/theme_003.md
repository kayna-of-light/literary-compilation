# Theme 003 — The direction of the law

## Swedenborg: one way

- "The action of the body … is only an effect from the internal and the external of man's spirit; for the body is only an obedient instrument" (*DP* §103).
- The natural world springs from the spiritual "precisely like an effect from its effecting cause" (*HH* §89).
- The order is conatus → force → motion, will united to understanding → forces → action (*DLW* §§218–219) (store 001).

## Delsarte: both ways

- "To each spiritual function responds a function of the body. To each grand function of the body corresponds a spiritual act."
- Waille: if psycho-spiritual phenomena flow into the body, "bodily manifestations also have a direct influence on the psycho-spiritual sphere" (store 003).
- Dalcroze's citation: "and vice versa" (store 010).

## The American layer: one way

- Stebbins: "The material form should correspond to the inner form"; "matter has no form of itself. It is being that forms the matter" (store 006).
- Northrop: every posture "is but an outward expression of an inner emotion or condition of the mind" (store 007).

## Stanislavsky: both ways

- *An Actor Prepares*: from the inner forces to the "physical creative instrument" (store 012).
- His later method of physical actions works from the outside in.
- Larson's quotation: "In every physical action … there is always something psychological and vice versa" (store 015).

## The 1937 studio: one way, by explicit choice

- Walt: "the mind is the pilot. We think of things before the body does them" (store 013).
- Graham: "it is the thought process that determines primary actions, not the action that determines the thought" (store 014).
- When Hilberman suggested in the 22 February class that "thought processes can be started by an action", the class's own answer was that "thought processes are started by the things around the actor", the perceived situation. Action as the cause of thought is not taught.
- Graham on 21 June: whether the artist "sees the force as the by-product of the drawing, or vice versa. It is the approach that is important."

## Reading

On direction, the studio's form of the law is Swedenborg's, not Delsarte's and not late Stanislavsky's. This fits the filter reading (theme_002): the layer that read the law in one direction, inner to outer, is the layer whose order the studio teaches.

This is a structural observation. The documents do not say the studio chose the direction *because* of Swedenborg.
