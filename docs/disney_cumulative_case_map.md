# The Disney–Swedenborg Cumulative Case: Evidence Map

**Purpose**: The `07_Cultural_Pneumatology` Disney documents argue a cumulative case: the studio carried Swedenborgian ideas to the public, and the evidence strands are each modest but converge. This map verifies each node of that case **once**, at its real strength, so that every document audit applies the same verdict and the case is stated as strongly as the evidence allows: no weaker, no stronger.
**Started**: 2026-10-16, at the author's request ("treat every document on this subject … keeping in scope the cumulation of the evidence and what it honestly points to").
**Hub document**: `data/07_Cultural_Pneumatology/The Parsimony Problem_ A Cumulative Case Analysis…` (five strands: documentary, pedagogical, biographical, structural, milieu).
**Status key**: **Verified** (checked against the record) · **Verified, narrower** (the record supports a smaller claim than the corpus states) · **Open** (not yet traced; no verdict) · **Not supported** (the record contradicts it, or the trail it rests on fails).

How to use: before auditing any `07` Disney document, check its claims against this map. When a document's audit verifies or overturns a node, update the node here in the same commit. The map records evidence; it never goes into a `data/` document.

## The case as a whole: what it honestly points to (running assessment)

- **Established by record**:
  - By 1948 the studio handled Swedenborgian material knowingly. It corresponded with a Swedenborgian church librarian about the film's spiritual content, and defended its angel, a departed human, "with sincere respect".
  - The studio's 1935–37 pedagogy carries a cause-to-effect structure (force before form, the mind as pilot) that the corpus reads as influx.
  - The same man, Adelquist, was head assistant director on *Snow White* and answered the 1948 letter.
  - *Snow White*'s ending is the studio's own invention. The production art shows it changed during development, from an earthly castle on a crag to a golden castle standing on clouds and radiating light.
  - A Swedenborgian congregation, the Church of the New Jerusalem, met at 509 South Westmoreland Avenue in Los Angeles throughout the years of *Snow White* and *Johnny Appleseed* (opportunity, not transmission).
- **Inference carried by convergence**: that this knowing engagement already shaped *Snow White* in 1937. This is the corpus's thesis, and it remains a convergence argument, as *Parsimony* §4.6 says itself.
- **Independence check** (internal consistency, `CLAUDE.md`): Adelquist appears in both the documentary and the biographical strands, so those two are not fully independent. *Parsimony*'s Appendix A now marks them a partial overlap (2026-10-17). The case still holds, but that convergence counts once, not twice.
- **Strengthened by the record (2026-10-19)**: Adelquist, who answered Murdoch in 1948, called Mary Blair a friend and close working associate of many years (1966 letter). The documentary node (1948) and the stylist of *Johnny Appleseed* are tied by a documented friendship.
- **Removed by the record (2026-10-20)**: Mary Blair's Swedenborgian confirmation and her board seat. The Wayfarers confirmand of 1973 was Mrs. William Blair of Torrance. The case loses its only documented personal-faith link inside the studio's creative staff; what remains for Blair is her work and her colleagues. Adelquist and Murdoch (1948) remain the documented point where the studio knowingly handled Swedenborgian material.
- **Removed by the record**: Blair's Palos Verdes residence and the Disney "residential cluster" (2026-10-19), Carmer on *Sleepy Hollow* (2026-10-19), the Adelquist–Jasper family link, the Retta Scott Worcester residues, and Ted Sears's part in rejecting *Chanticleer* (1960, after his death).
- **Strengthened by the record (2026-10-18)**: the three *Snow White* innovations the case leans on — the Queen's self-destruction, the kiss and the castle in the clouds — all stand together in one document, Creedon's tentative outline of 22 October 1934. The convergence the case argues is present in the studio's first written story, not assembled later.
