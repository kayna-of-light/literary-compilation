## **I. Introduction: The Phenomenological Battle for the Lord**

This report conducts an in-depth analysis of a central, irreconcilable conflict within non-orthodox spiritual frameworks. This is not a "spectrum" of belief, but a spiritual conflict between two paths that, "like clay and iron, they do not mingle" (Dan 2:41–43).  
This battle is not about religious dogma, but about the *experiential and doctrinal interpretation* of the "Lord in ultimates". The inquiry for this report is based on the framework articulated by Emanuel Swedenborg, which posits two key correspondences:

1. **The Lord as the "Sun in Heaven":** A universal, divine source of love and light from which all good and truth flows.  
2. **The "Divine Human in Jesus Christ":** The "expression of Him in ultimates," the pure, non-dogmatic, and most direct *identification* of this divine source as it is made manifest to humanity.

This analysis maps two structurally opposed orientations:

The first pole, the **"Path of Affirmation" (or Path of Love)**, centers on *reception*. It is defined by *affective* (emotional and relational) mystical encounters with the universal "Being of Light" (the "sun in heaven"), through which love flows from the Lord through the soul outward. The encounter produces transformation toward greater love of others, transcendence of self-claims, and dissolution of dogmatic boundaries. This path receives good and truth *from* the Lord. The identification vocabulary through which the receiver names this encounter — Jesus, Amida, light, an unnamed presence — is the Lord's accommodation to what is most intimate to that person; the structural marker is the *direction* of the result, not the name used.

The second pole, the **"Path of Self-Appropriation" (or Path of Gnosis)**, represents the claiming of the divine encounter as the self's own possession, status, or elevation. This path "establish[es] ourselves as the prime suspects for gnosis," rejecting the Lord as the *sole source* of good and truth. The criterion is structural, not nominal: this orientation can operate under any name — including the name of Jesus — and its structural signature is the multiplication of cosmological scaffolding centred on the self's special role or knowledge.

This "Path of Self-Appropriation" is *not* coherent in its cosmology. It is "very inconsistent" and presents "a thousand conflicting ways" to describe the divine. Its *only* coherent feature is its *consistent strategy* of dismantling the "Divine Human" correspondence through two primary tactics:

1. **Splitting the Identity:** Separating the human "Jesus" from an impersonal, universal "Christ" principle.  
2. **Changing the Name:** Explicitly replacing the ultimate identification of "Jesus Christ" with another, such as "Sananda" or "Jmmanuel".

## **II. The Path of Affirmation: The Lord as Divine Human (Jesus Christ)**

This section analyzes frameworks in which encounters with the "Lord as Divine Human" produce *reception* — love and truth flowing from the Lord through the soul outward. The path is defined by the *direction* of the encounter's result: transformation toward greater love of others, transcendence of self-claims, and independence from the receiver's prior religious framework. The NDE accounts examined here represent this path in its most immediate phenomenological form. The identification vocabulary the receiver uses reflects what is most intimate and accessible to them — it is the Lord's accommodation to each receiver, not the criterion by which the path is classified.

### **A. The NDE as Direct Phenomenological Evidence**

The most potent modern expression of the "Path of Affirmation" is found in the collected testimonies of Near-Death Experiences (NDEs). These accounts *directly* mirror the Swedenborgian correspondence:  
**1\. The "Sun in Heaven" (The Source):** The central, cross-cultural feature of NDEs is the encounter with a "very bright light" (Moody 1975, quoted in Zigarelli 2024). This "Being of Light" is described as an "ineffable, supreme being" (Zigarelli 2024) radiating "unconditional, absolute love" (Irene A., NDERF 2912). This is a perfect phenomenological match for the "sun in heaven".  
**2\. The "Divine Human in Ultimates" (The Identification):** When NDE experiencers identify the "Being of Light" in personal form, the identification reflects their cultural vocabulary. In Western, predominantly Christian datasets, a notable subset identify the figure as Jesus (King 2024) or the "Christ Light" — and this identification frequently transcends and contradicts the experiencer's prior institutional dogma (the "Dogma Paradox," §II.C). Crucially, the majority of non-Western experiencers identify the same being as a warm, all-encompassing presence consonant with their own tradition — a pattern cross-cultural NDE research confirms (see *The Being of Light: A Statistical Analysis of Near-Death Experience Phenomenology*). The encounter and its personality profile are the constant; the name is the receiver's cultural form.
This identification, wherever it falls, frequently *supersedes* religious dogma.

* **Non-Dogmatic Message:** The core message of this "Being of Light" is *never* institutional religion. It is *always* "unconditional love" and "non-judgment". The "Life Review" is described as self-judgment in the presence of that love: "This voice accepted me, and did not judge me. I, in a way, had judged myself" (Richard L., NDERF 3444). In one account, when an experiencer asked the Jesus he encountered what religion to be, he was simply told, "Follow your conscience" and "love your neighbor" (Catholic Answers 2024).  
* **Anomalous Cross-Framework Encounters:** A minority of NDErs from non-Christian backgrounds report identifying the figure as Jesus (Wise 2025; Rooted In Christ n.d.; Burke 2023; A Stronger Faith 2025). These cases confirm the universality of the encounter — that it transcends the receiver's prior framework — not that one identification form is ontologically privileged over others. Note that the available dataset is predominantly Western and Christian, which affects the baseline distribution of identification vocabulary; outlier identifications within this dataset should not be presented as the rule.

### **B. Pure Identification via Personality Match**

The consistency of this identification, even among non-believers, points to a "pure identification based on a personality match". The "Being of Light" in NDEs is not just an abstract illumination; it has a distinct, consistent, and *unique* personality. It is described as the source of "unconditional, absolute love" (Irene A., NDERF 2912), "knew everything about me" (Lisa M., NDERF 47), met the experiencer with "unconditional love, and total acceptance" (Patsy D., NDERF 307), and is non-judgmental ("we judge ourselves; God does not judge us"; Sharon B., NDERF 3670).  
This personality is a *perfect correspondence* to the "Divine Human" as He is revealed in the Gospels—a figure who refutes the *self's* (self-love's) desire for an external, physical kingdom and a "struggle for power," and whose message is the universal, internal "Path of Love". The "innate knowing" experiencers describe (Linda S., NDERF 2903) is real — it attests to a genuine encounter with the Lord's own nature as the Divine Human. What is recognised is the *quality*: unconditional love, non-judgment, total acceptance, the refusal of self-elevation. This quality is the Lord's constant self-expression (*DLW* §§11–13), an eternal attribute present to every soul through the form most intimate to that receiver. The name through which it is identified (Jesus, Amida, the Light) is the receiver's available vocabulary for what they meet — equally valid across all identification forms. The framework's position: the Lord in ultimates is the Lord's own eternal nature expressed in the human form; no historical individual owns this expression, and the data confirms its universality.

### **C. The "Dogma Paradox" and the NDE as *Corrective***

This NDE data reveals a "Dogma Paradox": the figure identified as Jesus delivers a message of universal, unconditional love that *directly contradicts* the exclusivist "faith alone" dogma of many religious institutions. This proves the experience is *not* a projection *of* dogma; it is an experience that *corrects* dogma. It empirically supports the premise that all good and truth is *received* from the Lord and that all are "fully capable of this love", rejecting the dogmatic "disconnect" that denies this "inflow into our very soul".

## **III. The Path of Gnosis: Dismantling the 'Divine Human' Correspondence**

This section maps the frameworks that actively work to *dismantle* the "pure identification" of the "Lord in ultimates". This "Path of Gnosis" is defined by its *cognitive* nature. This path is "very inconsistent" and its *only* coherent feature is its *consistent strategy* of denial.

### **A. Tactic 1: Splitting the Identity (The Gnostic Blueprint)**

The primary tactic is the *separation* of the fallible human "Jesus" from the divine, impersonal "Christ" aeon or principle.

* **Ancient Gnosticism:** The Valentinian Gnostic model states the divine "Savior" (the Christ aeon) descended upon the human Jesus at his baptism and *withdrew* from him before the crucifixion, leaving the human part to suffer alone (Irenaeus, *Haer.* 1.7.2). This shatters the "Divine Human" correspondence. Jesus is not the Lord, but the "saved savior" (Thomassen 2006, quoted in McCoy n.d.)—a *model* for others to achieve gnosis.  
* **Theosophy:** This blueprint was modernized by Theosophy. Blavatsky stated that Jesus was a "philosopher and moral reformer" (Blavatsky 1877) but separated this man from the "Christos," the *impersonal* divine principle *indwelling all humans* (Blavatsky 1889). Later Theosophists taught that the "Christ" was a *separate entity* (the Lord Maitreya) who "occupied the body of" the adept Jesus (Leadbeater 1925).  
* ***The Urantia Book***: This text performs the *exact same* Gnostic "split". It *dismantles* the "Divine Human" correspondence by "splitting the divine itself". It states that the "Eternal Son" is the *actual* second person of the Trinity, but that the man Jesus was the incarnation of "Michael of Nebadon". Crucially, it claims this "Michael" is *not* the "Eternal Son" but just *one of more than 700,000* "Creator Sons" (*Urantia Book* 21:1.4).

### **B. Tactic 2: Changing the Name (The ET/Ascended Master Elaboration)**

This is the most direct assault on the "pure identification" of the "Lord in ultimates," as it explicitly *changes the name*.

* **Billy Meier (Pleiadian):** Meier's *Talmud Jmmanuel* gives the "Lord's Prayer" in what it presents as its original form, addressed not to the Father but to the praying person's own spirit ("My spirit, you exist within omnipotence"; *Talmud Jmmanuel* 6:12), and the contact notes name its teacher **"Jmmanuel, who has wrongly been called Jesus Christ"** (Osborn 2009). This narrative claims "Jmmanuel" was a prophet in a line including Enoch, Elijah, Isaiah, Jeremiah, and Muhammad, with Meier himself as the seventh (Meier 2001).  
* **Ascended Master Teachings / Ashtar Command:** This is the most pervasive example. In these teachings, "Master Jesus" is almost universally referred to by his "galactic name," **"Sananda"**. This "Sananda" is placed in a hierarchy (the "Great White Brotherhood"; Hoeller 2023) and given a job title, "Commander-in-Chief" of the Ashtar Galactic Command's flying saucer fleet (Tuella 1985).

## **IV. Analysis of "Hybrid" Systems as Sophisticated Gnosis**

The systems that appear to be "hybrids" are, upon closer inspection, more sophisticated expressions of the "Path of Gnosis". They *use* the language of the "Path of Affirmation" to deliver a Gnostic message of self-glorification, thereby "establishing ourselves as the prime suspects for gnosis".

### **A. A Course in Miracles (ACIM)**

*A Course in Miracles* is a prime example of this sophisticated Gnostic strategy.

* **Gnostic Tactic:** It *uses* the *authority* of the "Path of Affirmation" by claiming to be dictated by Jesus himself (Center for A Course in Miracles n.d.).  
* **Gnostic Message:** It uses this authority to deliver a *purely Gnostic* message: that the world is an "illusion" of "separation" and that "Christ" is not Jesus, but the *impersonal, shared identity* of the *entire* "Sonship" (all of creation) (*A Course in Miracles* T-2; Center for A Course in Miracles n.d.).  
* **Analysis:** This is the *exact same* Gnostic "dismantling" tactic seen in the *Gospel of Philip* ("is no longer a Christian but is Christ"; *Gos. Phil.* 67.26–27, trans. Scopello and Meyer 2008) and in Joseph Campbell's New Age summary (the idea that "God was in Christ" is "true of *you and me as well*"; Campbell 1988, quoted in Groothuis 1990). The personal "Jesus" is used as a vehicle to teach that *we are all* the "Divine Human."

### **B. The Law of One (Ra Material)**

*The Law of One* employs a similar Gnostic tactic, using "love" as the content of its Gnosis.

* **Gnostic Tactic:** It *uses* the *language* of the "Path of Affirmation" (Love, Oneness) but *contextualizes* it within a Gnostic hierarchy of "densities".  
* **Gnostic Message:** It *dismantles* the "Divine Human" correspondence by reframing Jesus as just a "Wanderer" from the 4th density (*Ra Material* 17.11–12). He is merely a *teacher* of the "Law of One", an adept of "understanding or love", not the "Lord in ultimates".

## **V. Comparative Analysis: Coherency and Framework Refinement**

These two paths cannot be reconciled on an "axis." They are "clay and iron". A direct comparison reveals their fundamental, incompatible natures.

* **Path of Affirmation (NDE):** This path possesses high *affective and message-based* coherency. The message of unconditional love is simple, powerful, and consistent across thousands of accounts. It requires *minimal cosmological scaffolding*. Its authority is the self-validating *experience* of the "Lord as Divine Human".  
* **Path of Gnosis (Dismantling):** This path is *cosmologically incoherent* and "very inconsistent". It is an "umbrella term" for "a thousand conflicting ways". Theosophy, *Urantia*, and Ascended Master teachings present "conflicting messages" (Helland 2003) with wildly different hierarchies, reincarnation lists, and definitions. Its *only* coherent feature is its *strategy*: the consistent, unified goal of *dismantling* the "Divine Human" correspondence and "establishing ourselves as the prime suspects for gnosis".

## **VI. Conclusion: The Incompatible Paths of Reception and Denial**

The "clay and iron" metaphor is the correct and final framework. The "Path of Affirmation" and the "Path of Gnosis" are not two different "axis points" on a "spectrum"; they are fundamentally incompatible spiritual realities.  
**The "Path of Affirmation"** is one of *reception*. It is based on the spontaneous, affective *experience* of the "Lord as Divine Human" — the Lord's constant self-expression as unconditional love, non-judgment, and total acceptance. Its authority is phenomenological, and its message is *coherent and simple*: love from the Lord, flowing through the soul outward. This path receives good and truth *from* the Lord. The personality profile confirmed across thousands of NDE accounts corresponds to the Lord's own eternal nature as the Divine Human. The identification vocabulary through which experiencers name this encounter (Jesus, Amida, light, presence) is the Lord's accommodation to each receiver's most intimate form of knowing; the constant is the quality, not the name.  
**The "Path of Gnosis"** is one of *denial*. It is based on the *cognitive* rejection of the Lord as the *sole source* of good and truth. This path "establish\[es\] ourselves as the prime suspects for gnosis" and "prevent\[s\] His light from reaching" humanity by *denying His very "being in ultimates"*.  
This "Gnostic framework" is "very inconsistent". The data confirms it is a "cornucopia of controversy" (Groothuis 1990), a collection of "conflicting messages" and "a thousand conflicting ways"—from Gnostic aeons, to Theosophical Masters, to the 700,000 Creator Sons of the *Urantia Book*, to the "Sananda" of the Ashtar fleet.  
This inconsistency is not a flaw in its system; it *is* the system. The "Path of Self-Appropriation" is a "Babel" of conflicting cosmologies whose *only* unifying, coherent feature is its *structure*: the self claiming what flows through it as its own possession, status, or elevation. This structure can operate under any name and produces cosmological multiplication in every case.  
This framework also clarifies the NDE data itself. The overwhelmingly positive NDEs are not a *survey* of the general state of humanity, but are "biased towards the message"—a form of divine influx given to "guides of light" to "ignite change". The "hellish" NDEs, like Pittman's, are the *diagnosis* of the general state: a "correspondial vision" of a world dominated by the "97.5%" (Pittman 1983; Williams n.d.) who follow the *self* of "faith alone" and "actively cut off the divine".
