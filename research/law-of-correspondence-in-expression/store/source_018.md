# Source 018 — *Tikkunei Zohar*, "Patach Eliyahu": the feature test, Kabbalistic side

**Citation**: *Tikkunei Zohar*, Second Introduction ("Patach Eliyahu"). English translation by David Solomon (2024), Sefaria (CC-BY-NC).

**Accessed**: 2026-10-24 (Sefaria API, text read).

## Passage

"And they are called in this arrangement: Ḥesed – right arm, Gevurah – left arm, Tipheret – body, Netzaḥ and Hod – two thighs, and Yesod – end of the body, – sign of the holy covenant. Malkhut – 'mouth' – we call it 'Oral Torah'. The brain is Ḥokhmah, it is inner thought. Binah is the heart, and with it the heart understands."

## Comparison of body maps

| Zone | Delsarte (store 002; Northrop's summary, store 007) | Zohar | Swedenborg's Greatest Man (*HH* §§65, 96) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head | Mind, intellect ("the eyebrow is the thermometer of the mind") | "The brain is Ḥokhmah, it is inner thought" | Love, peace, innocence, wisdom, intelligence: the highest heaven |
| Torso | The moral, affection, love | Binah the heart; Tiferet the body | Charity and faith: the middle heaven |
| Limbs | Life, power, the will ("the thumb is the thermometer of the will") | Ḥesed and Gevurah the arms; Netzaḥ and Hod the thighs | The arms and hands are "in the power of truth from good"; the feet in natural good |

## Reading

- Delsarte's placement of the mind in the head and of love in the torso matches the Zohar's brain as thought and heart as understanding.
- Swedenborg puts love and innocence in the head, the highest of the three heavens. On the head, Delsarte's map is Kabbalistic, not Swedenborgian.
- On the limbs as power (Delsarte's "the limbs, power", in Northrop), Swedenborg's arms "in the power of truth from good" agree.

A detailed body-correspondence system older than Swedenborg was available in Delsarte's documented training (Kabbalah through illuminist freemasonry; Waille 2015 ¶36, store 004).

## Why it matters

There is no feature in Delsarte found only in Swedenborg. The detail of his law does not require Swedenborg, and it does not come from him.

In the framework's terms, the Zohar's body and Swedenborg's Greatest Man are two witnesses to one body-correspondence knowledge. *Mundus Imaginalis* §5.2 tabulates Adam Kadmon and the Maximus Homo; *Transmission of the Ancient Word* §VII.D describes Kabbalah as a "parallel development with different organization".
