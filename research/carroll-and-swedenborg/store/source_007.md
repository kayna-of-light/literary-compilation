# Source 007 — Reis, *George MacDonald* (1972)

**Citation**: Reis, Richard H. *George MacDonald.* Twayne's English Authors Series. New York: Twayne, 1972.
**Accessed**: 2026-10-21, archive.org FTS `georgemacdonald00reis`.

## Findings
- MacDonald "apparently read widely in the classic literature of mysticism, including Swedenborg and Boehme".
- On one of MacDonald's symbols: "a striking similarity between MacDonald's use of this symbol and that of Blake in *Jerusalem*" — derived "from Blake or from one of Blake's chief sources for such lore, Boehme or Swedenborg".

## Why it matters
A standard literary-critical monograph that places Swedenborg among MacDonald's reading. Hedged ("apparently"); source_005 supersedes it with MacDonald's own words.
