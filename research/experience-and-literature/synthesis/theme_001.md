# Theme 001 — Experience and the literature built on it: what the published record supports

This synthesis tests the five points of `plan.md` against sources 001–013. Each point gets a verdict: **supported**, **partly supported**, **not supported**, or **untested**. No personal experience of the author is used.

## 1. Spontaneous experience and produced material differ in how they arise and what they contain — **supported**, with a necessary qualification

- **How they arise.**
  - Channelling is a practice: taught ("a natural skill easily learned"), entered on purpose, run on audience questions (source 008).
  - In the largest survey of trance channelers, most describe the process as "willful and consensual" and are consciously aware of it (source 006).
  - Spontaneous OBEs arise mostly in relaxation, near sleep or near death. Most people have them once or a few times, and most cannot produce them at will (source 013 and the agent's report from Alvarado's Studies 2–3).
- **What they contain.**
  - The ordinary spontaneous OBE stays in ordinary surroundings. Entities appear in 18% of the British sample. No study codes for cosmologies, named beings or addresses, because the accounts do not carry them (source 013).
  - In the project's NDE archive, named stars appear in 9 of 6,768 raw files. Three of these tie a being or the self to a star, and in two of the three the label came later (*From Planets to Constellations* §3.1; `structured-data-analysis/projects/nde/scripts/star_name_search.py`).
  - Channelled texts supply full cosmologies, origins and missions (*From Planets to Constellations* §2).
- **Qualification.** The distinction is about mode and content, not about whether anything real happens in channelling.
  - Channelers are not as a group disordered (source 006); mediums are no more dissociative than non-mediums (source 012).
  - Some controlled studies report results their authors judge inconsistent with fakery (source 012).
  - Swedenborg's own position is that contact is real and its content unreliable (*Spiritual Diary* §1622; *Heaven and Hell* §249).
- **Correction to the 2026-11-02 document.** It sorted experiences as "forced" or "unforced." The better line is *produced on demand* versus *arriving unasked*. Seeking to understand an experience that is already happening is not producing it.

## 2. Experiencers carry their own uncertainty, and the labels come later — **supported**

- **Accounts are stable.** NDE scale scores were unchanged over nearly two decades: "not embellished" (source 010).
- **Names and frameworks arrive afterwards, from reading.**
  - "Ten years of bewilderment before finally reading an article that named the phenomenon"; "it named it so then I could speak in proper terms" (source 005).
  - Two OBE experiencers name a Reader's Digest article read 6 and 10 years later (source 013).
  - Prior knowledge does not predict OBE content (r = .08), and features may generate belief (source 013).
- **Doubt is recorded at the time.** "I was thinking, 'This cannot be. I cannot be in two places'" (Alvarado case SC1001, via the agent). Systematic data on uncertainty are thin. Strong reality judgments are the norm (94% "more real than a dream," Gabbard & Twemlow 1984, via the agent).
- **Swedenborg shows the same two steps.** In the *Diary*: "I am not sure" (*SD* §1670). In the published book: an earth in the starry heaven (*EU* §§148–149). The description of the spirits holds; the label moves (source 001).

## 3. Direction of change in the self — **partly supported**; the decisive comparison is **untested**

- **Supported.**
  - After NDEs, people value material and social success less and care more for others. This is corroborated by significant others and persists two decades (source 009).
  - Spiritual superiority, correlated with communal narcissism, is highest among people trained in "energetic" paranormal skills and goes with "self-ascribed spiritual guidance" (source 007).
  - Channelled teaching carries the "you are God" axis (source 008).
- **Complicating.** The same NDE studies report "increased self-worth," and an early broad survey found "a feeling of special importance or destiny" (source 009).
- **Untested.** No study measures narcissism in spontaneous experiencers against channelers or trainees.
- **Defensible statement.** Values shift away from status after spontaneous experiences. Superiority is highest where spiritual skills are trained. A direct comparison does not yet exist.

## 4. Stars as signs, read as places — **supported** in Swedenborg's own texts

- In the key, stars are "the knowledges of good and truth," not the stars (*AC* §§2495, 2849, 4697). Around angels, little stars appear, "fixed with those who are in genuine truths … wandering with those who are in falsified truths" (*AR* §51) (source 002).
- In the same body of work, Swedenborg's natural philosophy placed peoples on earths around the stars (*EU* §§123–178), and moved one described group there from Jupiter (source 001).
- The library's literalization thesis names the move: "the heavens have been spatialized into locations" (*Reversing the Arrow* §5.1).
- **Caution.** The play on planets as "wandering stars" and falsities as "wandering stars" is not evidence (source 002).

## 5. Spontaneous experience pointing to checkable things — **not supported** as proof; the contrast holds only on the literature side

- **Prospective hidden-target tests during cardiac arrest: none positive.** AWARE II: "Nobody identified the visual image" (source 004).
- **The best-documented dream case, Hilprecht's, was true and checkable.** Its own investigator traced it to the dreamer's own unconscious reasoning on material he had (source 003).
- **Where channelled content can be checked, it carries the receiver's library and era, and its dated predictions fail** (source 011). Positive mediumship studies exist and are disputed (source 012).
- **Defensible statement.** The literature's factual claims fail where they can be checked. Spontaneous experience has well-documented anecdotes but no controlled demonstration that it brings in information from outside. The argument must not claim more.

## What this means for *From Planets to Constellations*

1. Replace "forced versus unforced" with "produced on demand versus arriving unasked." Cite sources 006, 008 and 013.
2. Add the two-step pattern (the experience holds still; the name arrives later) from published sources 005, 010 and 013, beside the archive search already in §3.1.
3. Add Swedenborg's own move from Jupiter to a star system (source 001). It is primary evidence for the thesis from inside his texts.
4. State the direction-of-change evidence at its real strength (sources 007, 009), including what complicates it.
5. State plainly that controlled tests of veridical perception have not succeeded (source 004). The argument does not rest on them.
6. Keep the stars as signs (source 002) as the reading the key gives. The literal addresses are the reading of the letter.

## Library issues found along the way (for the author)

- *The Empty Room* §16 uses a single first-person account as an "experiential falsification." This is the use of a researcher's own or an unsourced personal account that the author has asked to avoid. Flagged, not changed.
- *The Empty Room* §14 states the Theosophy → channelling → starseed lineage without citation. Brown 1997 and Hanegraaff 1996 (source 008) could supply published support.
- *The Selfhood of the Prophet* lists four instances of anchoring. Source 001 documents a fifth, spatial, instance with the same shape.
