# Source 001 — Lovett, *Lewis Carroll among His Books* (2005)

**Citation**: Lovett, Charles C. *Lewis Carroll among His Books: A Descriptive Catalogue of the Private Library of Charles L. Dodgson.* Jefferson, NC: McFarland, 2005.
**Accessed**: 2026-10-22/23, archive.org full-text search, item `lewiscarrollamon0000love` (lending-only; read by phrase-probing).

## What it is
The fullest catalogue of Dodgson's library. Its core is the catalogue of the Oxford auction that began 10 May 1898 (E. J. Brooks, Holywell Music Room), supplemented by later auction and bookseller catalogues and surviving books.

## How complete it is — Lovett's own words
- The Brooks Catalogue "describes 1545 titles in 2711 volumes, and these titles form the core of the present catalogue. Unfortunately, there are nearly as many items listed in the Brooks Catalogue as merely 'others' or 'bundle of books.'"
- "because of the practice of the auctioneer of lotting up groups of undescribed books at the May 10 auction, we can never know for sure that Dodgson did not own a particular book."

**Consequence**: the catalogue can show that Dodgson owned a book; it cannot show that he did not.

## Entries that touch Swedenborg (index: "Swedenborg, Emmanuel 669, 1289, 2267")
- **669.** Emerson, Ralph Waldo. *Representative Men. Seven Lectures.* London: G. Routledge & Co., 1850. (Lovett's note: after the opening essay on great men, "the other six are devoted to Plato, Swedenborg, Montaigne, Shakespeare, Napolean, and Goethe".)
- **1289.** Mahan, Asa. *Modern Mysteries, Explained and Exposed, in Four Parts … IV. Clairvoyant Revelations of Emmanuel Swedenborg.* Boston: Jewett; Cleveland: Jewett; New York: Sheldons; London: Trübner, 1855.
- **2267.** Wilkinson, James John G. *The Human Body and its Connexion with Man, Illustrated by the Principal Organs.* London: Chapman and Hall, 1851. Lovett quotes Emerson on him: "Wilkinson, the editor of Swedenborg, the annotator of Fourier, and the champion of Hahnemann".
- No work by Swedenborg himself (full-text searches for *Heaven and Hell*, *Arcana*, *Coelestia*, *Divine Love*, *Divine Wisdom*, *Angelic Wisdom*, *New Jerusalem*: no hits).

## Related entries the library had not used
- **1852.** Sinnett, Alfred Percy. *Esoteric Buddhism.* London: Chapman and Hall, 1885. **1853.** Sinnett, *The Occult World* (London: Trübner).
- **1903–1904.** Society for Psychical Research, *Journal* vols. 1–7 (1884–97) and *Proceedings* vols. 1–11 (1882–95). Lovett: "Dodgson was a charter member of the Society for Psychical Research."

## Why it matters
It is the record for any claim about what Carroll read. It supports "Swedenborg reached Dodgson's shelves through Emerson, Mahan and Wilkinson"; it does not support "Dodgson owned Swedenborg's works", and its gaps forbid "Dodgson did not own them".
