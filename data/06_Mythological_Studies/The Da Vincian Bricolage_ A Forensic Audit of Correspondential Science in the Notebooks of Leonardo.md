## **Section III: The Physics of Spirit – Force, Influx, and the *Mēnōg***

### **3.1 Force as "Spiritual Essence"**

In the Codex Atlanticus, Leonardo defines force in terms that would be heretical to a strict materialist but are axiomatic to a Swedenborgian.

"Force is spiritual essence which by fortuitous violence is united to weighty bodies... Force is a spiritual, incorporate and invisible power." 16

**Forensic Analysis:**

* **"Incorporate and Invisible":** Leonardo defines Force as belonging to the *Mēnōg* realm. It is "invisible" and "incorporeal." It is not matter; it is the *animator* of matter.  
* **"Spiritual Essence" (*Virtù Spirituale*):** He explicitly uses the term "spiritual." In the framework, this validates the concept that all motion in the physical world is the result of spiritual influx. Matter is dead (inert); only Spirit (Force) moves.  
* **"Fortuitous Violence":** Leonardo describes force as "violence" done to a body to remove it from its "natural inclination" (inertia/gravity).  
  * **Theological Parallel:** In Swedenborgian theology, the "natural inclination" of the human *Self* is gravity—the pull towards self and hell (the earth). **Regeneration** is the "violence" (Force) done to this natural will by the influx of Divine Truth, compelling it to move in a new, spiritual direction. Leonardo's mechanics of force are a perfect allegory for the mechanics of regeneration.

### **3.2 The Genealogy of Motion: A Trinitarian Physics**

Leonardo writes: "Force is the child of physical motion and the grandchild of spiritual motion".17 This establishes a three-tiered hierarchy of causality that mirrors the Swedenborgian doctrine of **Discrete Degrees** (End, Cause, and Effect).

1. **Grandparent: Spiritual Motion (*Mēnōg* / End):** This is the Prime Mover, the Divine Will, or the "Love" that initiates all things. It is the "unmoved mover" in the spiritual world.  
2. **Parent: Physical Motion (*Influx* / Cause):** This is the transmission of that Will into the realm of action. It is the "Wisdom" or "Method" of the Divine Bricolage.  
3. **Child: Force (*Gētīg* / Effect):** This is the localized manifestation of that motion in a physical body. It is the "Use" or the "Work" performed in the material world.

**Insight:** Leonardo did not view mechanics as separate from theology. His physics was a "Genealogy of the Spirit." He traced the lineage of a lever's movement back to its "grandfather"—the Spiritual Motion of the universe.

### **3.3 The Vortex: The Shape of Influx**

Leonardo was obsessed with the **Vortex** (*la voragine*). He drew spirals in water, in hair, in the flight of birds, and in the movement of wind.11

* **Swedenborgian Correspondence:** Swedenborg describes the flow of spiritual influx as "gyres" or spiral movements. The "Spiritual Sun" emanates its heat and light in vortical forms.  
* **The Transition State:** Leonardo identified the vortex as the shape of **Force** encountering **Resistance**. It is the geometry of the interface between the *Mēnōg* (fluid/spiritual) and the *Gētīg* (fixed/material). By mapping the vortex, Leonardo was mapping the precise point where Spirit enters Matter.
